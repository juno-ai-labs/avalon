Build a page for one event in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, tell me how to connect it, then carry on. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which event is this, and what is its date?” and “Which vendors are you using, or should I find them in your mail?”

Show the days to go until the date at the top. List every vendor with its state: asked, quoted, booked, deposit paid, or confirmed for the day. Read each whole thread, my replies included, and move a vendor on when an email says so, such as a quote, a booking, a deposit receipt or an arrival time. Count how many vendors are confirmed for the day. Link every vendor to its thread, and go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Add two lists. One holds what the client has asked for, newest first, with what each request changes, such as a new headcount. The other holds what is due before the date, soonest first: payments, confirmations and anything I promised.

Fill the page from recent mail now and attach a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. Use an incoming-mail trigger if the connection supports one. If it only supports scheduled checks, state the actual refresh cadence. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same page, say what each of them needs to do and show who is running the event. Keep the page within this Canvas's audience.
