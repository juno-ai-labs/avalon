Build a page for one event in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which event is this, and what is its date?” and “Which vendors are you using, or should I find them in your mail?”

Show the days to go until the date at the top. List every vendor with its state: asked, quoted, booked, deposit paid, or confirmed for the day. Read each whole thread, my replies included, and move a vendor on when an email says so, such as a quote, a booking, a deposit receipt or an arrival time. When a vendor is dropped or turns the job down, mark it dropped and leave it out of the count. Count how many vendors are confirmed for the day. Link every vendor to its thread, and go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Add two lists. One holds what the client has asked for, newest first, with what each request changes, such as a new headcount. The other holds what is due before the date, soonest first: payments, confirmations and anything I promised.

Fill the page from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the page really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the page is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same page, say what each of them needs to do and show who is running the event. Do not share the page with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
