Build a docket of open legal requests in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which requests should count: contract reviews, NDAs, anything sent to legal?” and “Which domains are colleagues, and which are the other side?”

Make one numbered line per matter. Show the matter, who asked for it, its stage, whose turn it is, and for how many days. A matter is always with exactly one of three: legal, the business, or the other side. Read each whole thread, my replies included. When an email passes the turn, such as answers from the requester or comments from the other side's counsel, move the matter and start its day count again. Above the list, count the open matters and how many sit with each of the three. When a matter is signed, withdrawn or otherwise finished, mark it closed, take it off the open list and leave it out of the counts. Link every line to its thread.

Track requests only. Do not review, summarize or give an opinion on any contract, and go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Fill the docket from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the docket really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the docket is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same docket, say what each of them needs to do and show whose inbox each matter came from. Do not share the docket with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
