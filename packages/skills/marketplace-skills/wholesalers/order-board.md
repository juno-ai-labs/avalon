Build an order board in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which customers or senders send you orders?” and “What do you sell by: cases, pallets, units?”

Make one card per order or quote request, in four columns: New, Quoted, Confirmed and Shipped. Show the customer, the quantity, the amount when the thread states one, and the day it is due. Read each whole thread, my replies included, so a quote I sent counts. Move a card when a later email changes its stage, such as an approval, a purchase order or a shipping notice, and link every card to its thread. Show the number of cards and the total amount for each column. When an order is cancelled or a quote is turned down, mark the card lost, take it off the board and leave it out of the totals. Go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Under the board, list quotes that have had no answer, oldest first, with the days since I sent them.

Fill the board from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the board really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the board is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same board, say what each of them needs to do and show whose inbox each card came from. Do not share the board with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
