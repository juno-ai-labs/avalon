Build a client document checklist in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which clients is this for, and what period?” and “Which documents does each client owe you?”

Make a grid with one row per client and one column per document. Tick a box when an email from that client delivers that document, going by what the email says and the names of its attachments. Do not judge whether a document is complete or correct. Show how many of its documents each client has sent, and mark a client ready when every box in its row is ticked. Link each tick to the email that earned it.

Under the grid, list who still owes what, with how many times I have asked and when I last asked. Read my sent mail for that. If I ask for reminders, write them as drafts and leave them in my Gmail Drafts for me to send.

Fill the checklist from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the checklist really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the checklist is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same checklist, say what each of them needs to do and show whose client each row is. Do not share the checklist with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
