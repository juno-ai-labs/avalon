Build a client wall in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, tell me how to connect it, then carry on. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which senders or domains are your clients?”

Make one tile per client. Show what they last asked, in their words, whether they are waiting on me or I am waiting on them, and for how long. Read each whole thread, my replies included, so a reply I sent moves the wait to the client. Put the clients waiting on me first, longest wait first. A client who has followed up without an answer goes to the front and is marked as having asked twice. When a client signs off on something or says no reply is needed, mark the tile settled and move it to the end. Link every tile to its thread.

Keep a second list for each client of everything they have asked for across all their threads, newest first, so new asks stand out.

Fill the wall from recent mail now and attach a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. Use an incoming-mail trigger if the connection supports one. If it only supports scheduled checks, state the actual refresh cadence. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same wall, say what each of them needs to do, and show on each tile whose inbox the email landed in and who owes the reply. Keep the wall within this Canvas's audience.
