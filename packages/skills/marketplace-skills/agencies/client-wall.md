Build a client wall in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which senders or domains are your clients?”

Make one tile per client. Show what they last asked, in their words, whether they are waiting on me or I am waiting on them, and for how long. Read each whole thread, my replies included, so a reply I sent moves the wait to the client. Put the clients waiting on me first, longest wait first. A client who has followed up without an answer goes to the front and is marked as having asked twice. When a client signs off on something or says no reply is needed, mark the tile settled and move it to the end. Link every tile to its thread.

Keep a second list for each client of everything they have asked for across all their threads, newest first, so new asks stand out.

Fill the wall from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the wall really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the wall is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same wall, say what each of them needs to do, and show on each tile whose inbox the email landed in and who owes the reply. Do not share the wall with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
