Build a school notification list and calendar in this Canvas from messages sent to my email. Ask only for details you need. “Which connected mailbox or label should I watch?” and “Which schools, senders, or school mailing lists should count?” If the calendar time zone cannot be determined from the source or my profile, ask which time zone to use.

Make one notification per distinct announcement, deadline, or event. Link to the original emails and update the same row when the school sends a repeat, correction, or reschedule. Let each viewer mark a notification as seen and preserve that viewer's seen state when another email updates the same item.

Build a calendar from the school dates in those messages. Capture events, deadlines, closures, and schedule changes, preserving all-day dates and the local time zone. Move or cancel the existing event when an update identifies it. Keep announcements with unclear dates in the notification list until the dates can be confirmed.

Populate the list and calendar from matching emails and set up a Sidekick to handle new messages. Use incoming-mail events if the connection supports them, or state the actual polling cadence. Keep school messages and family details within the Canvas's authorized audience.
