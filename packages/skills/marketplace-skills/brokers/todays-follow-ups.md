Build a page of today's follow-ups in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Are you in real estate, mortgage or insurance?” and “Which senders are leads or clients, such as a website form or a listing site?”

Make a list called Today with one line per person waiting on me. Show who they are, what they asked, and how long they have waited. Put new leads at the top, then whoever has waited longest. Mark anyone who has asked twice. Read each whole thread, my replies included, and take a line off the list when I have answered or an appointment is booked. Link every line to its thread.

Under it, show where each deal, loan file or policy stands, in the words of my trade, with its next date. Change a date when an email changes it, and say what it was before. Go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Fill the page from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives and sends me a push notification when a new lead writes. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the page really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the page is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same page, say what each of them needs to do and show whose lead each line is. Do not share the page with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
