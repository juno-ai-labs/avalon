Build a page of today's follow-ups in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, tell me how to connect it, then carry on. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Are you in real estate, mortgage or insurance?” and “Which senders are leads or clients, such as a website form or a listing site?”

Make a list called Today with one line per person waiting on me. Show who they are, what they asked, and how long they have waited. Put new leads at the top, then whoever has waited longest. Mark anyone who has asked twice. Read each whole thread, my replies included, and take a line off the list when I have answered or an appointment is booked. Link every line to its thread.

Under it, show where each deal, loan file or policy stands, in the words of my trade, with its next date. Change a date when an email changes it, and say what it was before. Go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Fill the page from recent mail now and attach a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives and sends me a push notification when a new lead writes. Use an incoming-mail trigger if the connection supports one. If it only supports scheduled checks, state the actual refresh cadence. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same page, say what each of them needs to do and show whose lead each line is. Keep the page within this Canvas's audience.
