Build a recruiting market map in this Canvas. Ask me these two questions if I haven't already answered them. “What role are you hiring for?” and “Which company are you hiring for?”

Research the hiring company in depth using current public sources. Cover what it sells, its customers, business model, stage and scale, locations, and recent developments relevant to the role. Read its own website, job listings, and public filings or announcements where available. Explain how the company's needs shape the candidate profile, and distinguish sourced facts from inferences.

Create a ranked market map of relevant employers and teams, including direct competitors and adjacent companies with transferable experience. For each, give the likely role titles, geography when supported, why the experience transfers, and links to the evidence. End with practical search terms and any gaps that would change the ranking.
