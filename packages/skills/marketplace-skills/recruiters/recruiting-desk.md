Build a recruiting desk in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, show me how to connect it and build nothing until it is. Ask me to reply here once it is connected. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which roles are you working, and for which clients?”

Group the page by role. Under each role, make one row per candidate I have introduced to that client. Show the candidate, the client contact, the stage (Submitted, Screen, Interview, Offer, Placed), whose reply the intro is waiting on, and for how many days. Read each whole thread, my replies included. Move a row along when an email from either side changes its stage, and link every row to its thread. When a candidate is turned down or withdraws, mark the row closed and leave it out of the days waiting and the quiet list. Count the placements this month at the top.

Add a list of who has gone quiet: clients who have not answered a submittal and candidates who have not answered me, by days of silence. Track the conversations already in my mail. Do not look for new candidates, and go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Fill the desk from the last month of mail now, and set up a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. A reply I send does not arrive as new mail, so have the Sidekick also check on a schedule for mail I sent, and tell me how often the desk really updates. Store the date each wait or deadline started, and work out every count of days from the current time, so the numbers are right whenever the desk is opened. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same desk, say what each of them needs to do, show whose candidate each row is, and flag a candidate two of us are both writing to. Do not share the desk with anyone or make it public. I will choose who sees it.
