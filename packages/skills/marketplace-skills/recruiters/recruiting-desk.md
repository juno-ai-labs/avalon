Build a recruiting desk in this Canvas from my email. If Gmail is not connected yet, tell me how to connect it, then carry on. Ask only for what you cannot work out from the mail. “Which roles are you working, and for which clients?”

Group the page by role. Under each role, make one row per candidate I have introduced to that client. Show the candidate, the client contact, the stage (Submitted, Screen, Interview, Offer, Placed), whose reply the intro is waiting on, and for how many days. Read each whole thread, my replies included. Move a row along when an email from either side changes its stage, and link every row to its thread. Count the placements this month at the top.

Add a list of who has gone quiet: clients who have not answered a submittal and candidates who have not answered me, by days of silence. Track the conversations already in my mail. Do not look for new candidates, and go by what each email says and the names of its attachments.

Fill the desk from recent mail now and attach a Sidekick that updates it as new mail arrives. Use an incoming-mail trigger if the connection supports one. If it only supports scheduled checks, state the actual refresh cadence. If I name teammates whose inboxes should feed the same desk, say what each of them needs to do, show whose candidate each row is, and flag a candidate two of us are both writing to. Keep the desk within this Canvas's audience.
