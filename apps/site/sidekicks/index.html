01Sidekicks

The AI that keeps work current.

A Sidekick is the helper behind a page that updates itself. It watches your email and your other tools, or runs on a schedule. When something changes, it does the work: updates the page, drafts the follow-up, posts the digest. Every new one starts as yours alone. Share it, and your team talks to it in one conversation.
