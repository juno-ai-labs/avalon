06 / Questions

A few things
worth knowing.

Is Rally another chat client?＋

No. Conversation is one part of a workstream. People, connected sources, AI activity, questions, and outputs stay attached to the outcome.

Does everyone need the same setup?＋

No. Rally is designed to make a strong setup shareable. Invite someone into the workstream and the relevant context and work trail are already there.

Can I see what Rally did?＋

Yes. Tool activity, sources, questions, outputs, failures, and retries remain inspectable in the workstream.

Where can I use Rally today?＋

Rally currently ships as a macOS desktop product for Apple silicon. This site is a product tour, not a browser version of the workspace.
