Questions, answered

What Avalon is—and isn’t.

Is Avalon another chat client?＋

No. Conversation is one part of a work stream. Participants, connected resources, agent activity, questions, and outputs remain attached to the outcome.

Does Avalon work without people involved?＋

Avalon can continue assigned background work, but it brings decisions back to people when judgment or missing context matters.

What can trigger an automation?＋

Automations can run manually, by calendar or interval, from connected activity, by watching a resource, through a webhook, or from email.

Where can I use Avalon today?＋

Avalon currently ships as a macOS desktop product for Apple silicon. This landing page is a product tour, not a browser version of the workspace.
