Technology

Built multi-player from day zero.

Technology designed to work with multiple humans from information permissions to turn detection to connector delegation

  1. 01

    LLM routing core

    Through thoughtful design we’re able to keep a facade model for a consistent voice but delegate work to families of models best suited to their strengths

  2. 02

    Authorization

    Multiple humans means multiple permissions and data visibility. We’ve thought through edge cases and expectations to protect information without getting in the way of getting work done.

  3. 03

    Connectors

    Gmail, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, and more. Rally reads what you point it at and writes back where your team works.

  4. 04

    Sidekicks

    Sidekicks are a way of letting a single AI enhance itself on-demand as tasks require it, a different ‘hat’ for every task.

  5. 05

    Multiplayer collaboration

    With a core bet that groups of humans will continue to be the core of work we’re on a mission to create 100x teams not just 100x individuals.
