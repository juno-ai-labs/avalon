questions.

More curious? There’s a whole manual.

What’s the difference between a Canvas and a Sidekick?+

A Canvas is a conversation and the things Rally makes in it. A Sidekick is a set of instructions for ongoing work. Give it a schedule or an event to react to, and it can run on its own and write back to a Canvas.

Can I use Rally on my own?+

Absolutely. Start is free, with the same Canvases, Sidekicks, and connected tools. Your Canvas conversation starts private; invite editors when you’re ready. Artifacts have their own access settings, so you can share an output without sharing the conversation.

How do usage and paid plans work?+

Every plan has one usage allowance for your Organization, allocated weekly across work in Canvases and Sidekicks. Start is free. When you need more room, an administrator can choose Grow, Max, or Ultra in Organization Usage settings. Paid-plan overages are optional and billed separately.

Is there a trial?+

Your Organization’s trial starts when it is created: 14 days, with no credit card needed to begin. Bring two coworkers before it ends to extend it to 60 days in total. Start remains free after the trial.

Where can I use Rally?+

Rally works in your browser, with desktop apps for macOS and Linux. Your Canvases and Sidekicks stay with your Organization, so you can pick up your work across devices.

Does my team have to switch tools?+

Keep the tools you already use. Connect Rally to tools like Slack, Linear, Gmail, Notion, Google Drive, and GitHub so it can read the context you need and write back where you work. Explore the connectors.
