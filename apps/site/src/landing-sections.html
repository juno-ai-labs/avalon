00Why Rally

Every tracker, report and dashboard starts to go stale the day it’s written. Someone has to keep it current, and usually nobody does. Rally keeps them live, and keeps your team in them.

01Reactive artifacts

Made once.
Kept current from then on.

Ask Rally for a document, a spreadsheet, a dashboard or a site, then say what should keep it current. When something happens in your tools, Rally reads it and publishes a new version of the same Canvas. The link stays the same, and the page stops going stale.

Here is the loop on its own. Three things happen in three tools over a few days. Each one starts a run of the Sidekick, which reads what changed and publishes the next version of the tracker. Drag through it, or let it play.

Nobody pasted the Linear status into a doc or retyped the new date from Slack. The tracker is simply right. Now put the team in it: the same page, with the Chat beside it, where people ask Rally about the latest version and leave comments on it.

07Technology

Built multiplayer
from day zero.

Technology designed to work with several people at once, from who can see what to when Rally should speak to whose access a connector uses.

  1. 01

    LLM routing core

    One consistent voice up front, with the work handed to the family of models best suited to it.

  2. 02

    Authorization

    Several people means several sets of permissions. We protect information without getting in the way of the work.

  3. 03

    Connectors

    Gmail, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub and more. Rally reads what you point it at and writes back where your team works.

  4. 04

    Sidekicks

    Shared agents that run on a schedule or when something happens, with their own tools, delegated access and the Canvases they write to.

  5. 05

    Multiplayer collaboration

    Our core bet: groups of people stay at the center of work. We want 100x teams, not just 100x individuals.
