04 How it’s built

Built for many people from line one.

Adding a second person to an AI chat is not a feature you bolt on. It changes who may see what, who is speaking, and whose access a job runs with.

  1. 01

    Each person sees their own

    Rally keeps track of who can see each file, message and memory, so sharing a Canvas never shares more than you meant.

  2. 02

    It knows who’s talking

    Three people in a conversation is not one long prompt. Rally follows the room and answers the person who asked.

  3. 03

    Access is delegated, not borrowed

    A Sidekick runs with access someone chose to give it, and every run says whose it used.

  4. 04

    One voice, many models

    Each job goes to the model family best at it. You hear one Rally.
