03Sidekicks

The recurring work,
done for all of you.

A Sidekick is an AI your whole team shares. Ask it something and it starts a run everyone can watch, right where you asked.

  1. Every Monday 9:00

    On a schedule

    Every hour, every day or any schedule. It checks, acts and reports.

  2. A call ends

    When something happens

    An event in a connected tool or a webhook starts a run. A follow-up after every sales call, say.

  3. Needs input

    Asks only for what only you know

    A Sidekick never waits for permission to use a tool. It asks when it’s missing something only a person has.

  4. Canvas written

    Hands back a Canvas

    What it makes lands in a Canvas your team can open, comment on and keep working in.

05Technology

Built multiplayer
from day zero.

Technology designed to work with several people at once, from who can see what to when Rally should speak to whose access a connector uses.

  1. 01

    LLM routing core

    One consistent voice up front, with the work handed to the family of models best suited to it.

  2. 02

    Authorization

    Several people means several sets of permissions. We protect information without getting in the way of the work.

  3. 03

    Connectors

    Gmail, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub and more. Rally reads what you point it at and writes back where your team works.

  4. 04

    Sidekicks

    Shared agents that run on a schedule or when something happens, with their own tools, delegated access and the Canvases they write to.

  5. 05

    Multiplayer collaboration

    Our core bet: groups of people stay at the center of work. We want 100x teams, not just 100x individuals.
