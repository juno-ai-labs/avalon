Documents that stay live.

A Canvas is a document, spreadsheet or site Rally builds from your tools. Tell it what should keep it current, and it publishes the next version when an email lands, a pull request merges or a call note arrives. It starts as yours; share it when the team needs it.

01 How it works

Ask for it. Wire it up. Let it run.

Nobody types into a Canvas directly. Rally makes each change and publishes it as a version, whether a person asked or something happened in a connected tool.

  1. Ask for the thing

    “Build a pipeline sheet from my last month of customer email.” Rally reads what you point it at and makes the first version beside the chat.

  2. Say what keeps it current

    “Update it whenever a customer replies or a call note lands.” Rally sets up a Sidekick to watch your connected tools and publish the next version.

  3. Steer it any time

    Ask for a change or comment on a cell. It lands in the conversation, and the next version answers it.
