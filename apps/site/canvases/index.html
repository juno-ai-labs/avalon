01Canvases

Made once.
Kept current.

A Canvas is a document, spreadsheet, dashboard or site, and the conversation that made it. Say what should keep it current, and Rally publishes a new version whenever something changes in your tools. Start one on your own, and share it when others need it.

02How it works

Four moves.
No setup.

01 · Ask, on your own

Say what you need.

Start a private Canvas and ask in plain words. Point Rally at a meeting’s notes, a Drive folder or a thread in Slack, and it reads them.

MayaBuild a hiring tracker from the candidates in my inbox.
RallySearched GmailRead 14 emails

02 · Rally makes it

The thing itself, not a reply about it.

Rally makes the artifact in the Canvas and publishes a new version each time it changes it, so you can see what moved.

Hiring trackerSpreadsheet · v1
Hiring trackerSpreadsheet · v2, added a stage column

03 · Keep it live

Tell it what to watch.

“Update this whenever a candidate replies.” Rally sets up a Sidekick that watches Gmail and publishes a new version of the tracker each time, marked with the Sidekick’s name.

GmailAna Ruiz replied: “Thursday at 2 works.”
Hiring trackerSpreadsheet · v7 · Updated by Hiring tracker

04 · Share it

Then everyone steers.

Share the Canvas and coworkers join the same Chat, with the whole history. Leave a comment on a line, a cell or a spot on the page, and Rally reads it too. Nobody pastes context into a private chat again.

RallyAdded them. Two candidates have no interview yet.
