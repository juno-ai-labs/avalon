01 How it works

Say it. Point at it. Share it.

Nobody types into a Canvas directly. People talk, Rally makes the change, and every version has an author and a reason you can scroll up to.

  1. Ask for the thing

    “Turn yesterday’s call into a launch plan.” Rally reads what you point it at and makes the first version beside the chat.

  2. Point at what to change

    Leave a comment on a line, a cell or a spot on the page. It lands in the conversation, and Rally answers it in the next version.

  3. Bring people in

    Start private. Add a teammate, invite a guest, or open the Canvas to the whole organization when it’s ready.

02 What Rally makes

Not a reply. The thing itself.

Rally makes the file you asked for, opens it in the panel beside the conversation, and keeps it there as the work moves on.

Documents

Plans, briefs, memos.

Written with the whole conversation in view, so nobody needs a recap.

Spreadsheets

Trackers and models.

Real cells you can comment on, and download when you need the file.

Sites

Live pages.

A published site with its own address, updated version by version.

Versions

Every state, kept.

The title stays, the card in the chat still points at it, and earlier versions stay readable. No “final (2) (really final)”.
