Rally vs Slack

Short answer: Keep Slack. It’s great for quick chat, and most teams aren’t leaving. Slack AI can summarize channels and find answers. Rally is where people and an AI do the actual work together: drafting, researching, planning, and building artifacts with shared context. You can mention @Rally in Slack and it reports back there, while the full history lives in Rally. Nobody has to copy answers from a chat window into Slack anymore.

Credit where due

Where Slack shines.

  • Everyone is already there

    Your team, your vendors, and half your customers.

  • A huge app directory

    Whatever tool you use, it probably posts into Slack.

  • Fast, casual chat

    For a quick question or a GIF, nothing’s faster.

Our side of the story

Where Rally wins.

  • Built for AI from day zero

    Slack came before AI teammates. Rally was built around one, with turn-taking, permissions, and tool use designed in from the start.

  • Context that stays put

    Slack threads scroll away. Rally Spaces keep the files, decisions, and history together, so the AI and the next person start caught up.

  • Artifacts that stay current

    Rally makes documents and live dashboards that stay with the project and keep up with it.

  • Works with Slack

    Mention @Rally, DM it, or type /rally. Progress shows up in Slack. Nobody has to give up their favorite emoji.

No offense to Slack, but we think your team could use a little less slack.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Pick Slack if

  • You only need team chat
  • Your AI needs are quick summaries and search
  • You need shared channels with outside companies

Pick Rally if

  • You want people and AI doing the work together
  • You want context that doesn’t scroll away
  • You want artifacts that stay with the project
  • You want to keep Slack and add a real AI workspace

Questions

Rally vs Slack, answered.

Do I have to leave Slack to use Rally?

No. Install the Rally app in Slack, then mention @Rally, DM it, or use /rally. Rally posts progress back to Slack and keeps the full history in Rally, where your team can pick it up with all the context.

Isn’t Slack AI enough?

Slack AI is good at catching you up: summaries, recaps, and search. Rally is for doing the work: drafting with teammates, pulling from Gmail, Drive, Notion, and GitHub, building documents and dashboards, and running Sidekicks on a schedule.

How does Rally pricing compare to Slack?

Slack charges per seat. Rally charges per organization: $100 a month on Start and $250 a month on Grow, with no seat limit. Most teams keep both.
