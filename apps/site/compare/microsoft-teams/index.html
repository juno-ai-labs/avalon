Rally vs Microsoft Teams

Short answer: Microsoft Teams is chat and meetings for companies that run on Microsoft 365, and Copilot adds AI on top, usually as a per-seat add-on. Rally is a simpler bet for small teams: one shared place where people and one AI work together, one price per organization with no seat limit, and connectors for Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, GitHub, and more.

Updated October 1, 2026 · Written by the Rally team. Yes, we’re biased. We tried to keep it fair anyway.

Credit where due

Where Microsoft Teams shines.

  • Meetings

    Teams does meetings well, with transcripts and recaps built in.

  • Deep Microsoft 365 ties

    If your company runs on Outlook, SharePoint, and Excel, Copilot knows where everything is.

  • IT already said yes

    It’s probably on every company laptop already.

Our side of the story

Where Rally wins.

  • Small-team pricing

    One price per organization and no seat limit. No per-seat AI add-on to budget for.

  • Google-friendly

    Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar connect in a couple of clicks, along with Slack, Notion, and GitHub.

  • Breakouts for side work

    Need to sort something out with one coworker and the AI? Break out, then merge it back into the Channel when you’re done.

  • Fast to start

    Download, invite two coworkers, connect your tools. No admin center tour required.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Pick Microsoft Teams if

  • Your company runs on Microsoft 365
  • You need meetings and chat in one app
  • IT requires Microsoft tools

Pick Rally if

  • You’re a small team that wants AI working with everyone
  • You use Google Workspace, Slack, or Notion
  • You don’t want a per-seat AI add-on
  • You want to start this afternoon

Questions

Rally vs Microsoft Teams, answered.

Does Rally work with Microsoft 365?

Rally’s connectors today focus on Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear, and other tools small teams use. If you run on Microsoft 365, tell us what you need. We read every email.

Is Rally a replacement for Teams meetings?

No. Rally isn’t a video call tool. It’s where the work around the meeting happens: it reads meeting notes from Granola or Fireflies, drafts follow-ups, and keeps decisions with the project.

How much does Rally cost compared to Teams with Copilot?

Rally is $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, AI included, and neither plan counts seats. Teams and Copilot are each priced per seat.
