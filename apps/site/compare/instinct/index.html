Rally vs Instinct

Short answer: Instinct is a personal agent for everyday tasks like booking travel, ordering groceries, and managing subscriptions, over text or voice. It’s built for one person’s life. Rally is built for a team’s work. Your coworkers and one AI share Spaces and Channels, connect the tools you use at work, and get projects done together. Different jobs. Use Instinct for your weekend and Rally for your week.

Updated October 1, 2026 · Written by the Rally team. Yes, we’re biased. We tried to keep it fair anyway.

Credit where due

Where Instinct shines.

  • It runs your errands

    If you hate admin, handing off travel and groceries is easy to love.

  • Text or talk

    You can message it like a very capable friend.

  • It finishes the job

    It doesn’t just suggest a flight. It books it.

Our side of the story

Where Rally wins.

  • Made for working with people

    Rally puts coworkers and the AI in one Channel, so plans, drafts, and decisions are visible to the people who need them.

  • Permissions for many humans

    A personal vault works for one person. At work, Rally tracks who can see which files and threads, so a shared AI respects everyone’s access.

  • Team projects

    Spaces hold a project’s files and history. Sidekicks handle the follow-ups, digests, and reports that keep it moving.

  • You can start today

    No waitlist. Download the Mac beta and start a 14 day trial with no credit card.

Rally won’t do your grocery run, but lettuce help with the launch plan.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Pick Instinct if

  • You want help with personal errands
  • You have an invite
  • The work is yours alone

Pick Rally if

  • You need AI for team projects
  • You want coworkers in the loop
  • You want permissions that respect each person
  • You want to start today

Questions

Rally vs Instinct, answered.

Can Rally book travel or order groceries?

That’s not what we built it for. Rally is for team work: drafts, plans, research, follow-ups, and dashboards, with your coworkers in the loop. For groceries, a personal agent or a good old shopping list still works fine.

Is Rally invite-only?

No. Anyone on a Mac can download the beta and start a 14 day free trial without a credit card. Invite two coworkers and we’ll extend it to 60 days.

Can I use Rally on my own?

You can, and Rally is good at solo work. It really shines when two or more people share a Space, which is why bringing two coworkers to your trial earns you the longer one.
