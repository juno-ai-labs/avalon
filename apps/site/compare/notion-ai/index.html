Rally vs Notion AI

Short answer: Notion AI is great if your team’s work already lives in Notion. It writes, summarizes, and runs agents across your pages and databases. Rally starts from the conversation instead of the page. Your team and one AI work together in Channels, and Rally works with Notion alongside Gmail, Drive, Slack, GitHub, and more.

Credit where due

Where Notion AI shines.

  • Writing inside the doc

    Drafting, rewriting, and summarizing right on the page is smooth.

  • Good with databases

    If your projects live in Notion databases, Notion AI knows its way around them.

  • One less tool

    If you’re all in on Notion, it’s already there.

Our side of the story

Where Rally wins.

  • Conversation first

    Work usually starts as a discussion. In Rally, three people and the AI talk it through, and the doc comes out the other end.

  • Beyond Notion

    Rally reads Gmail, Drive, Slack, GitHub, and your meeting notes too, so the answer isn’t limited to what someone remembered to write down.

  • Sidekicks do the follow-up

    Weekly digests, post-call follow-ups, and status checks run on their own and land in the Channel.

  • Flat price

    One price per organization, no seat limit.

Notion AI is great at taking notes. Rally is great at taking note of who’s doing what. We’ll see ourselves out.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Pick Notion AI if

  • Your whole team lives in Notion
  • You mostly need help writing inside docs
  • You want AI in your databases

Pick Rally if

  • Your work spans more tools than Notion
  • You want several people and one AI in the same conversation
  • You want follow-ups that run on their own

Questions

Rally vs Notion AI, answered.

Does Rally replace Notion?

No, and it doesn’t need to. Rally connects to Notion and works with your pages. Many teams keep Notion as the wiki and use Rally to do the work that ends up there.

Can Rally work with my Notion pages?

Yes. Connect Notion for yourself and Rally uses Notion’s own tools on your behalf. Connect it for the workspace and Rally sees only the pages an admin shares with it, which keeps private pages private.

What does Rally do that Notion AI doesn’t?

Rally puts several people and one AI in the same live conversation, with shared context across all your tools, private Breakouts, and Sidekicks that run on a schedule. It’s built around the team.
