Method

How we compare.

Every page asks the same eight questions, so you can line them up. We read each product’s public pages, say where it’s strong, then make our case. Prices and features change fast, so each page carries the date we last checked.

  • Who is it for?

    One person, or a team working together.

  • Can people share one AI conversation?

    Several humans and one AI in the same thread, all seeing the same thing.

  • Does context stick around?

    Files, decisions, and history that the AI and the next person can pick up.

  • Is there room for private work?

    Side work you can do alone or in a small group, then bring back.

  • Does it work with your tools?

    Reading from and writing back to the apps your team already uses.

  • Does it run on its own?

    Jobs on a schedule, or when something happens.

  • Which models?

    One vendor’s model, or the right one for each job.

  • How is it priced?

    Per seat, per credit, or per organization.

Questions

Questions, answered.

What is Rally?

Rally is a shared place where your team and one AI work together. Spaces and Channels keep each project’s files and history, Breakouts give you private side work, connectors read and write your tools, and Sidekicks run jobs on a schedule. It costs $100 or $250 a month per organization, with no seat limit.

How is Rally different from other AI tools?

Most AI tools give each person their own chat. Rally gives the team one, built from day zero for several people and one AI in the same conversation, with permissions that respect each person.

Who writes these comparisons?

The Rally team, so yes, we’re biased. We check each one against the other product’s public pages and update them as products change. If we got something wrong, email help@rallywith.ai and we’ll fix it.
