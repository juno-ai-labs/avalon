Rally vs Viktor

Short answer: Viktor is an AI employee you hire into Slack or Teams. You send it tasks, it uses a long list of integrations, and you pay in credits for what it runs. Rally puts the AI and your team in the same room. Everyone sees the same context, works on the same drafts, and passes work back and forth with the AI. You pay one flat monthly price for the organization, so nobody watches a credit meter.

Updated October 1, 2026 · Written by the Rally team. Yes, we’re biased. We tried to keep it fair anyway.

Credit where due

Where Viktor shines.

  • A huge integration list

    If your team uses something obscure, Viktor may already connect to it.

  • Slack or Teams, your pick

    It works in either one, so there’s no new app to roll out.

  • Good at big errands

    One message can turn into a full report, dashboard, or campaign.

Our side of the story

Where Rally wins.

  • Doing it together

    Handing off a task works. Doing it together works better. In Rally three people and the AI can work one draft in the same Channel, and everyone sees how it got there.

  • No credit meter

    Credits make people ration their questions. Rally’s plans are flat per organization, so people ask freely instead of counting.

  • Work has a home

    Spaces and Channels keep files and decisions together. Breakouts let you work privately and merge back.

  • Artifacts you can share

    Rally builds documents and full public dashboards that live with the project, not as a link at the bottom of a thread.

We thought about pricing Rally in credits, but we couldn’t take credit for the idea.

Verdict

Which one should you pick?

Pick Viktor if

  • You want an AI that takes tasks and reports back
  • You need a niche integration today
  • Your team lives in Microsoft Teams

Pick Rally if

  • You want the team and the AI working in the same context
  • You want a price that doesn’t move with every question
  • You want projects with Channels and private Breakouts
  • You want shareable documents and dashboards

Questions

Rally vs Viktor, answered.

Is Rally an AI employee like Viktor?

Not quite. Rally is a shared place to work where one AI is part of the team. You can hand it tasks, but the bigger win is working together: people and the AI see the same context, edit the same drafts, and pick up where someone else left off.

How does Rally pricing compare to credits?

Rally charges per organization, not per task. Start ($100 a month) suits teams under five and Grow ($250 a month) suits teams under fifteen, but neither one counts seats. Each plan has one shared quota. Bigger teams can talk to us.

Does Rally work with Microsoft Teams?

Not today. Rally connects to Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, GitHub, Linear, and more. If your team runs on Teams, tell us. We’re small and we ship fast.
