Avalon for Slack

A durable agent where your team already works

Delegate a task from an Agent DM, mention, shortcut, or /avalon. Avalon keeps the durable work history in your regional workspace and sends progress and results back to the originating thread.

Workspace installation enables the Agent, joins public channels so @Avalon is available, and supports scheduled DM delivery. Optional personal authorization enables Slack search and user-attributed actions through Slack’s hosted MCP server. Avalon does not subscribe to ordinary channel messages, scrape, or mirror Slack workspaces.
