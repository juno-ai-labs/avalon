Rally for Slack

Sidekicks that work where your team already talks

A Sidekick can watch the Slack channels you choose and start a run when something is said there, then post its results to a channel or as a direct message. Reply in the Slack thread under a Sidekick’s message and the same run carries on with your reply. Mentions and direct messages to Rally do not start work on their own; the run history stays in the Sidekick’s conversation in Rally.

Workspace installation adds the Rally bot, joins the public channels a Sidekick watches, and supports Slack activity triggers and Sidekick delivery to channels and direct messages. Optional personal authorization enables Slack search and user-attributed actions through Slack’s hosted MCP server. Rally may receive bounded Slack activity for the triggers your Sidekicks are configured with, but it does not scrape, mirror, or recreate Slack as a parallel inbox, channel browser, or searchable workspace.
