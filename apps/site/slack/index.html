Rally for Slack

A durable agent where your team already works

Delegate a task from an Agent DM, mention, shortcut, or /rally. Rally keeps the durable work history in your regional workspace and sends progress and results back to the originating thread.

Workspace installation enables the Agent, joins public channels so @Rally is available, and supports Slack activity triggers and scheduled DM delivery. Optional personal authorization enables Slack search and user-attributed actions through Slack’s hosted MCP server. Rally may receive bounded Slack activity for tasks and configured automations, but it does not scrape, mirror, or recreate Slack as a parallel inbox, channel browser, or searchable workspace.
