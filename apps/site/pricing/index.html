FAQ Questions

Fair questions.

Why per organization and not per seat?

Rally is for working together. A seat price makes every invite a budget decision, so the person who should read along never gets added. One price means everyone is in the room.

Can I use Rally on my own?

Yes. Everything starts private to you, and Start is priced for one person up to a small team. When you add people later, the price stays the same.

How does the free trial work?

Start a 14 day trial with no credit card. Invite at least two teammates and we’ll stretch it to 60 days.

What is the shared quota?

Each plan comes with one pool of AI usage for the whole organization, rather than a ration per person. Grow’s pool is larger than Start’s, and Grown up sizes it to your organization.

Can guests join a Canvas?

Yes. Invite someone from outside your organization to a single Canvas, without giving them anything else.

Where does Rally run?

On your Mac as a desktop app, and in any modern browser at app.rallywith.ai.
