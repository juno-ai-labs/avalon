Cookie & Tracking Notice

A plain account of what Avalon stores on your device and what our public site currently does not do.

Status · No advertising trackersVersion · 2026.08

1. Current position

Avalon’s public website does not currently set non-essential cookies or use advertising, behavioral-analytics, or cross-site tracking technology. Because there are no optional tracking cookies to accept or reject, we do not display a consent banner.

If that changes, we will update this Notice and, where required, ask for consent before setting non-essential cookies.

2. Website requests and operational logs

When you request a page, our hosting and security infrastructure receives technical information needed to deliver and protect it. This can include your IP address, user agent, requested URL, referral information sent by your browser, timestamps, and security or error signals. These are server and network logs, not advertising profiles.

We use this information to route traffic, prevent abuse, investigate errors, maintain availability, and comply with legal obligations. We do not use it to follow you across unrelated sites.

3. Avalon desktop storage

The Avalon desktop application may store limited preferences on your device, such as interface dimensions and device-level settings, using local storage or equivalent application storage. These preferences are not browser cookies and are not used for cross-site advertising. Authentication secrets and connector credentials use separate protected mechanisms described in our Security Policy.

4. Links and connected services

Avalon links to and can connect with services operated by other companies. Those services may use their own cookies, SDKs, pixels, logs, or similar technologies under their own notices. When you open a provider’s site or authorize a connection, review that provider’s choices and controls. This Notice does not govern their independent tracking.

5. Browser signals and privacy choices

Some browsers send “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signals. Since the public Avalon site does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use optional tracking cookies, there is no advertising sale or tracking operation to disable in response. We will honor legally applicable browser signals if our practices change.

You can still clear site data, block cookies, or limit referral information using your browser or device settings. Blocking all storage may affect future features if we introduce functions that require it.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this Notice when our site or desktop storage changes. We will change the effective date and provide a consent control if required. Questions can be sent to privacy@with-avalon.com. For broader data practices, see the Privacy Policy.
