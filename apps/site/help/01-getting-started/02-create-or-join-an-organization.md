---
title: Create or join an Organization
description: Start a new Organization for your team, join an existing one, or open a Canvas someone shared with you.
---

Everything you do in Rally happens inside an Organization, your team's home. Straight after you sign in, Rally shows **Your organizations**: the ones you belong to, with a count beside any that has something new for you. Click one to open it.

If the list is empty, Rally says **Ask a teammate to share a Canvas or a Sidekick with you, or create a new organization.** Which of those you want depends on how you got here.

- If your team already uses Rally, ask them to [invite you](#joining-with-an-invitation).
- If someone shared one Canvas with you, you do not need an Organization at all. See [Opening a Canvas shared with you](#opening-a-canvas-shared-with-you).
- If you are the first on your team, [create one](#creating-a-new-organization).

**Sign out**, at the bottom, is there in case you signed in with the wrong account. It happens to the best of us, usually the ones with a personal and a work account in the same browser.

## Creating a new Organization

1. Click **Create an organization**.
2. Type a name in **Organization name**. Your company or team name is the usual choice.
3. Click **Create organization**, or press <kbd>Return</kbd>.

The name needs at least two characters and can be up to 120. The button stays greyed out until the name is long enough. Pressing <kbd>Return</kbd> a few extra times out of impatience will not create a few extra Organizations. Click **Back** to leave without creating one.

You become the Organization's owner, and it opens straight away, empty and ready for your first Canvas. If you change your mind about the name later, an owner or administrator can rename it in **Settings → Organization** by double-clicking it. Its other settings are described in [Organization settings](../10-running-an-organization/03-organization-settings.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Older screens and emails may call an Organization a **Workspace**. They are the same thing.

## Joining with an invitation

When a teammate invites you, you get an email saying who invited you, to which Organization, and as what (a member or an administrator).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For now, accepting an Organization invitation needs the [desktop app](../08-rally-everywhere/04-the-desktop-app.md). The web app cannot accept one yet. Once you have joined, the Organization appears under **Your organizations** in the browser too, and you can do everything else there.

Click the invitation link in the email. Your browser opens a page headed **Continue in the Rally app**. Click **Open Rally**, and the app opens on that invitation. If the app does not open, install it first, then click the link again. If the page says **This invitation link is incomplete or invalid. Ask the sender for a new link.**, the link was damaged on its way to you, so ask for a fresh one.

You do not need the link at all, as long as you sign in to the app with the invited address. Rally finds invitations to your email on its own and lists them under **Choose where to join**. Each one shows the Organization's name, **Invited by** and the person's name, and the role you were offered: **Administrator** or **Member**.

- Click **Accept** to join.
- Click **Decline** to turn it down.

The person who invited you gets an email either way, so nobody is left wondering.

A few rules apply to every invitation.

- An invitation belongs to one email address. You must be signed in with exactly that address to accept it. If you are signed in as someone else, Rally says **This invitation is for** that address and asks you to sign in with that account.
- They expire after seven days, and each one can be used once. The email says exactly when yours runs out. If it has expired, ask the sender to invite you again.
- If accepting fails, you see **The invitation could not be accepted.** and the list refreshes. Try again, or ask the sender to check that the invitation is still open.

An invitation that reaches you after you are set up waits in the app's account menu, under **Invitations**. [Belonging to several Organizations](../10-running-an-organization/04-several-organizations.md) has more.

> [!TIP]
> If you only need to work on a few things with a team, and not join it, ask them to share those Canvases with you instead. That works entirely in the browser.

## Opening a Canvas shared with you

When someone shares a Canvas with you, you get an email with a link.

1. Click the link. Rally asks you to sign in.
2. Sign in with the email address the email was sent to.
3. The Canvas opens. There is nothing to accept: signing in with that address is all it takes.

- Signing in with a different address opens nothing. The share belongs to the address it was sent to, so a forwarded email is no use to anyone else.
- If the share was withdrawn or has expired, signing in shows no new Canvas. Ask the person who sent it for a new one.
- If you already use Rally with that address, the Canvas is already in your list. The email is there so you know.

As a guest you see the Canvases shared with you and little else, which is the point. You do not get the guided setup on the next page. Skip ahead to [A tour of Rally](04-a-tour-of-the-window.md). [Sharing with people outside your Organization](../02-canvases/09-sharing-with-guests.md) covers the other side: how someone shares a Canvas, and how they take it back.

## What comes next

If you created the Organization, Rally now walks you through a short [guided setup](03-guided-setup.md): choosing how you look and inviting your team.

If you joined someone else's, you get one step of it, choosing how you look, which is described in [This is you in Rally](03-guided-setup.md#this-is-you-in-rally). Then Rally takes you into the Organization, and you can skip ahead to [A tour of Rally](04-a-tour-of-the-window.md).
