Rally runs in your web browser. There is nothing to download: open a page, sign in, and you are in.

## What you need

- A recent web browser. Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge all work, on a computer, a tablet or a phone.
- An email address. You can sign in with a Google or Apple account, or with a code sent to any email address. Whichever you use becomes your identity in Rally.

You do not need an invitation to sign in. Signing in does not put you into anybody's Organization either; that happens on the [next page](02-create-or-join-an-organization.md).

## Open Rally

Go to [app.rallywith.ai](https://app.rallywith.ai). The **Sign up now** buttons on [rallywith.ai](https://rallywith.ai) lead to the same place.

The page opens on **Welcome to Rally**, with **Your team, your work, and Rally. All in one place.** under it. There is no separate sign-up. If this is your first time, signing in creates your Rally account.

> [!TIP]
> Bookmark the page, or pin the tab. Rally is the kind of tab you keep open all day.

## Sign in

Pick one of the buttons.

### With Google or Apple

1. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**.
2. Choose your account and approve.
3. You come back to Rally, signed in.

### With your email address

1. Click **Continue with email**, type your address and click **Email me a sign-in link**.
2. The page changes to **Check your email**. Rally sends you an email titled **Sign in to Rally**, with a code and a link in it.
3. Type the code into **Sign-in code** and click **Continue**. Or open the link in the email and click **Continue** on the **Confirm your sign-in** page.

The code and the link each work once. If the email does not turn up, or you typed the wrong address, click **Use another email or request a new code** and start again. **Other sign-in options** takes you back to the Google and Apple buttons.

There is no password to remember. (A **Sign in with password** link also sits on the page, but only accounts Rally's team set a password up for can use it. If you have never been given one, you do not have one.)

### Staying signed in

Your sign-in works in every tab and renews itself while you use Rally. Leave Rally alone for a long time and it asks you to sign in again. Signing out in one tab signs you out in all of them, which is what you want on a shared computer. [Signing in, signing out and Lockdown mode](../11-privacy-and-security/01-signing-in-and-out.md) has the details.

## On a phone or tablet

The same page works on a phone. The sections of the app sit behind an **Open navigation** button, and an open Canvas takes the whole screen.

On an iPhone or iPad, add Rally to your Home Screen (tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**) and open it from there. It then opens like an app, and it can send you notifications, which Safari in an ordinary tab cannot. [Activity and notifications](../08-rally-everywhere/03-notifications.md) shows how to turn them on.

## Where your data lives

Rally does not ask where in the world to keep your data. Every Organization you create lives in Rally's single Global deployment. For how your data is handled, see [What Rally shares, and with whom](../11-privacy-and-security/02-what-rally-shares.md).

## There is also a desktop app

If you use a Mac with Apple silicon or a Linux computer, you can also install Rally as an app. It shows the same Organizations and the same work as the browser, and adds a few things a browser tab cannot do, such as a built-in browser beside your Canvas. You do not need it to follow this book. [The desktop app](../08-rally-everywhere/04-the-desktop-app.md) says what it adds and how to install it.

Once you are signed in, [create or join an Organization](02-create-or-join-an-organization.md).
