---
title: A tour of the window
description: Find your way around the Spaces rail, the Channels list, the conversation and the panels beside it.
---

Rally's window is laid out like a ladder you read from left to right. Each column narrows things down from the one before it: your Organization's Spaces, then the Channels in one Space, then one Channel's conversation. Glance across it and you always know where you are.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Spaces rail | Every Space in your Organization, plus your account menu at the bottom |
| Channels rail | The Channels in the Space you picked |
| Channel sidebar | The open Channel's main conversation, its Breakouts and what it holds |
| Conversation | The messages themselves, and the box you type in |
| Inspector panel | A panel on the right for files and the built-in browser, when you open one |

This page walks along the ladder one rung at a time. Each part has a chapter of its own later; here you only need to know what is where.

## The Spaces rail

The narrow column at the far left lists your Organization's Spaces, each with its own icon and colour. A new Organization has just one, **General**, so this rail looks a little lonely at first.

- Click a Space to open its overview and show its Channels in the next column.
- Hover over a Space for a card with its name. A Space the whole Organization can see is marked **Organization space** in that card.
- A number badge on a Space counts the conversations in it that need your attention, such as a question from Rally or a mention from a teammate. [Status dots and badges](../02-working-in-a-channel/06-status-dots-and-badges.md) explains every one.
- Drag a Space up or down to reorder the rail. The order is yours alone: it follows you to your other devices, and nobody else's rail changes. From the keyboard, select a Space and hold <kbd>⌥ Option</kbd> while pressing <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd>.

At the top of the rail, the **+** button (**New channel or space**) starts a new Channel or a new Space, and the **Search** button searches the whole Organization. You will use the **+** on [the next page](05-your-first-channel.md). Spaces have a full page of their own in [Spaces](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/01-spaces.md), and search is in [Search and links](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/06-search-and-links.md).

## The account menu

At the bottom of the Spaces rail is your avatar and name. (The dashed hexagon labelled **New Sidekick** just above it belongs to [Sidekicks](../07-sidekicks/index.md), much later in the book.) Click your name to open the account menu:

- **Restart to Update** appears at the top when a new version of Rally has downloaded and is ready. See [Keeping Rally up to date](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/04-updates.md).
- **Sidekicks** opens the Sidekicks window, covered in [Sidekicks](../07-sidekicks/index.md).
- **Settings** opens the Settings window. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> does the same from anywhere.
- **Log out** signs you out of Rally on this Mac.

If your account can belong to several Organizations, the menu also lists them under **Organizations**, with a check mark beside the one you are in, and any waiting **Invitations**. See [Belonging to several Organizations](../10-running-an-organization/04-several-organizations.md).

For help, the **Help** menu in the Mac menu bar has a link to the Rally website, and [Contacting support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md) tells you how to reach a person.

## The Channels rail

The next column lists the Channels in the Space you picked, under that Space's icon and name.

- Channels are listed alphabetically. You cannot drag them into your own order, but you can pin the ones you use most, and pinned Channels sit in a **Pinned** section at the top. [Arranging your Channels](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/02-arranging-channels.md) covers pinning, moving and archiving.
- Click a row to open that Channel. Hover for a moment to see its full name in a card.
- Right-click a row for a menu of things you can do to it. Right-click an empty part of the list to start a new Channel in this Space.
- Each row can carry small markers: a status dot, a count badge when something in the Channel needs you, the time of the latest unread activity, a globe if the whole Organization can see it, and a clock if Rally has a reminder or follow-up scheduled there (hover over the clock to see when).

A Space with no Channels says **No channels yet**. That changes on the next page.

## The Channel sidebar

When a Channel is open, a third rail appears beside it. This is the Channel's own table of contents.

- The first row is the Channel's main conversation.
- Below it come the Channel's **Breakouts**, alphabetically, and a **New breakout** row for starting one. [Breakouts](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/03-breakouts.md) explains them.
- Then **Artifacts** (documents and files Rally has made) and **Resources** (things brought in from your other tools). These sections appear only once there is something in them. See [Things Rally makes](../06-things-rally-makes/index.md).
- **Channel participants** opens the list of people in the Channel, covered in [People in the room](../02-working-in-a-channel/05-people-in-the-room.md).
- **Sidekicks** lists the Sidekicks attached to this Channel. See [Sidekicks in a Channel](../07-sidekicks/06-sidekicks-in-channels.md).

## The conversation

The widest column is where the work happens: the messages between you, your teammates and Rally, with the message box at the bottom. The header across the top shows the Channel's icon and name. The whole of [chapter 2](../02-working-in-a-channel/index.md) is about this part.

## The inspector panel

Some things open in a panel on the right-hand side of the window, so you can read them beside the conversation.

- **A single file or document**: files usually open first in a large preview over the window. Click **Open in a righthand sidebar** to dock it here instead, beside the chat. [Opening, docking and downloading](../06-things-rally-makes/02-opening-and-downloading.md) has the details.
- **The Browser**: links can open in a built-in browser right here. See [Research and the built-in browser](../04-working-with-rally/02-research-and-the-web.md).

Drag the panel's left edge to make it wider or narrower.

## Making room

Every rail can shrink to a strip of icons when you want more space for the conversation.

- Collapse a rail with the button at its foot: **Collapse spaces**, **Collapse channels** or **Collapse channel**. The same button expands it again.
- Resize a rail by dragging its right edge. Drag it narrow enough and it snaps to icons; drag a collapsed one wide and it opens again.
- Opening a Channel tucks the Spaces rail down to icons automatically, to give the conversation more room. Click a Space, or the expand button, to bring it back.

On a narrow window Rally collapses rails by itself, so the conversation never gets squeezed too thin. Until you widen the window, the expand button says **Widen the window to expand**, followed by the rail's name.

## Rally remembers where you were

When you quit Rally and open it again, it returns to the Space, Channel or Breakout you were last looking at, with your rail widths and your place in the conversation as you left them.

This memory belongs to this Mac; your other devices keep their own. If the Channel you were in has since been archived, or you no longer have access to it, Rally opens your most recent Channel instead.
