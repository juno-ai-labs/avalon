---
title: A tour of the window
description: Find your way around the rail, the sidebar, the home pages, an open Canvas and an open Sidekick.
---

Rally's window has a sidebar on the left and your work on the right. The sidebar is how you get anywhere: a narrow strip of buttons down its left edge, called the rail, and next to it the list for whichever button you picked. Whatever you open from that list fills the rest of the window.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The rail | **Canvases** and **Sidekicks** at the top, your avatar and **Settings** at the bottom |
| The sidebar | A **Pinned** group, then everything else in the section, newest activity first |
| A home page | The whole section on one page, with **New**, search and filter chips |
| An open Canvas | The conversation in the middle, and what Rally made in a panel on the right |
| An open Sidekick | Its shared conversation, with its details beside it |

This page walks through each part. Each one has a chapter of its own later; here you only need to know what is where.

## The rail

Two buttons run down the top of the rail, each an icon with its name under it. Click one and the sidebar beside it changes.

- **Canvases** lists the Canvases you can open. A red number on the button counts the ones with something new for you: a reply, a comment, a new version, or a mention.
- **Sidekicks** lists the Sidekicks shared with you. Its red number counts the ones waiting for you, chiefly those under **Needs input**.

At the bottom of the rail sit your avatar, which opens the account menu described [below](#the-account-menu), and **Settings**. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> opens Settings from anywhere.

The dock icon and the Mac menu bar show the same count as the rail.

## The Canvases sidebar

Click **Canvases** and the sidebar lists them.

- **Pinned** comes first, once you have pinned something. Drag a Canvas into it to pin it, and drag pinned Canvases up and down to put them in your own order.
- **All canvases** follows, with every other Canvas you can open, the one with the newest activity first.
- Each row shows the Canvas's mark, its name, and a line under the name. When people have the Canvas open right now, a green dot and their names appear there, such as **Maya and Theo are here**. Otherwise the line says who changed it last, such as **Edited by Rally**.
- A bold name and a red dot mean something is new in that Canvas since you last looked. Opening it clears both.
- Click a row to open the Canvas. Right-click it for **Pin** or **Unpin**.

[What a Canvas is](../02-canvases/01-what-a-canvas-is.md) has the whole story.

## The Sidekicks sidebar

Click **Sidekicks** and the sidebar lists the Sidekicks shared with you, in three groups:

- **Needs input**, at the top, holds any Sidekick that is waiting for something only a person can give it, such as which Slack channel a status update should go to. The row shows its question.
- **Pinned**, for the Sidekicks you pinned.
- **All sidekicks**, everything else, newest activity first.

Each row shows the Sidekick's hexagon, its name and what it is doing: **Working**, when it runs next, or **Paused by** whoever paused it. [The Sidekicks section](../03-sidekicks/05-the-sidekicks-section.md) has more.

> [!NOTE]
> "Needs input" means a Sidekick is missing information. It never means a Sidekick is waiting for permission to use a tool. Rally does not ask anyone to approve its tool calls.

To make the sidebar narrower or wider, drag its right edge.

## The home pages

Each section also has a home page, which shows the whole section at once and fills the window when nothing in the section is open.

The **Canvases** home page has a **New Canvas** button, a search box and filter chips: **Everyone**, **Made by me**, **Shared with me** and **From Sidekicks**. Below them, **Live now** lists the Canvases people have open at this moment, and **Recent** lists the rest. In a new Organization the page shows only **New Canvas** and a **Start from a prompt** card, which is the quickest way in, as you will see on [the next page](05-your-first-canvas.md).

The **Sidekicks** home page has **New Sidekick**, a search box and the filter chips **Everyone**, **Only you**, **Organization** and **Paused**. Each row shows what the Sidekick does, its status, who it is shared with and when it next wakes up.

## An open Canvas

Open a Canvas and it fills the window.

- At the top floats the Canvas's **header pill**, with its name. Click the name for the Canvas's menu: renaming, copying a link, and its other settings.
- To the right of the pill are the faces of the people who have the Canvas open right now, then **Share**, then a **…** menu.
- The conversation fills the middle: your messages, your teammates' messages and Rally's, with the message box at the bottom. It says **Ask Rally or the people here…**.
- Once Rally has made something, it sits in a panel on the right, with its version. Drag the panel's edge to make it wider or narrower.

The whole of [chapter 2](../02-canvases/index.md) is about this screen.

## An open Sidekick

Open a Sidekick and you see its one shared conversation, with a details column beside it. In the conversation, people talk to each other and to the Sidekick, each piece of work the Sidekick does appears as a folded row, and anything it made appears as a card that opens the Canvas. The details column shows its status, **Pause**, who it can act as, its schedule, its triggers, its tools and the Canvases it writes to. [Chapter 3](../03-sidekicks/index.md) covers all of it.

## The account menu

Your avatar at the bottom of the rail opens the account menu:

- **Restart to Update** appears at the top when a new version of Rally has downloaded and is ready. See [Keeping Rally up to date](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/04-updates.md).
- **Log out** signs you out of Rally on this Mac.

If your account can belong to several Organizations, the menu also lists them under **Organizations**, with a check mark beside the one you are in, and any waiting **Invitations**. See [Belonging to several Organizations](../10-running-an-organization/04-several-organizations.md).

For help, the **Help** menu in the Mac menu bar has a link to this manual, and [Contacting support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md) tells you how to reach a person.

## Rally remembers where you were

When you quit Rally and open it again, it returns to what you were last looking at, with your sidebar width, the panel you had open and your place in the conversation as you left them. The first time, or if that place has gone, it opens the Canvases home page.

This memory belongs to this Mac; your other devices keep their own.
