title: A tour of the window
description: Find your way around the rail, the sidebar, the home pages, an open Canvas and an open Sidekick.

Rally's window has a sidebar on the left and your work on the right. The sidebar is how you get anywhere: a narrow strip of buttons down its left edge, called the rail, and next to it the list for whichever button you picked. Whatever you open from that list fills the rest of the window.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The rail | **Canvases**, **Sidekicks** and **Activity** at the top, your avatar (**Settings**) at the bottom |
| The sidebar | The list for the section you picked: a **Pinned** group and everything else, newest activity first, or your Activity |
| A home page | The whole section on one page, with **New**, search and filter chips |
| An open Canvas | The conversation in the middle, and what Rally made in a panel on the right |
| An open Sidekick | Its shared conversation, with its details beside it |

This page walks through each part. Each one has a chapter of its own later; here you only need to know what is where.

## The rail

Three buttons run down the top of the rail, each an icon with its name under it. Click one and the sidebar beside it changes. Switching sections changes only the list; whatever you have open stays open.

- **Canvases** lists the Canvases you can open. A red number on the button counts the ones with something new for you: a reply, a new version, or a mention.
- **Sidekicks** lists the Sidekicks shared with you. Its red number counts the ones waiting for you: those under **Needs input**, and those with something new in their conversation.
- **Activity** lists what happened to you: mentions, replies, being added to a Canvas, Sidekick runs that need you or finished, and requests to open a Canvas. Its number counts the rows you have not seen yet.

At the bottom of the rail sits your avatar, labelled **Settings**. It opens the account menu described [below](#the-account-menu). <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> opens the Settings window from anywhere.

The Dock icon and the Mac menu bar add up the **Canvases** and **Sidekicks** numbers. Activity's number stays on the rail.

## The sidebar

The top of the sidebar names the section, with a **+** for **New Canvas** or **New Sidekick**, and a magnifying glass for **Search organization** ([Search and links](../02-canvases/11-search-and-links.md)). In Activity, the **+** is replaced by **Mark all seen**.

### Canvases

- **Pinned** comes first. Drag a Canvas into it to pin it, or right-click a row and choose **Pin**.
- **All canvases** follows, with every other Canvas you can open, the one with the newest activity first. The **Recent** menu beside the heading sorts it by **Name** instead.
- Each row shows the Canvas's mark, its name, and a line under the name. When other people have the Canvas open right now, a green dot and their names appear there, such as **Maya and Theo are here**. Otherwise the line says who changed it last, such as **Edited by Rally**, or who made it.
- A bold name and a red dot mean something is new in that Canvas since you last looked. Opening it clears both.
- Click a row to open the Canvas. Right-click it for **Pin** or **Unpin**, **Rename…**, **Share…** and **Copy link**.

[What a Canvas is](../02-canvases/01-what-a-canvas-is.md) has the whole story.

### Sidekicks

Click **Sidekicks** and the sidebar lists the Sidekicks shared with you, in three groups:

- **Needs input**, at the top, holds any Sidekick with a run waiting for something only a person can give it, such as which Slack channel a status update should go to. The row shows its question.
- **Pinned**, for the Sidekicks you pinned.
- **All Sidekicks**, everything else, newest activity first.

Each row shows the Sidekick's hexagon, its name and where it stands: **Working**, **Runs** and when it runs next, **Last run** and how long ago, **Active**, **Paused** or **Draft**. [The Sidekicks section](../03-sidekicks/05-the-sidekicks-section.md) has more.

> [!NOTE]
> "Needs input" means a Sidekick is missing information. It never means a Sidekick is waiting for permission to use a tool. Rally does not ask anyone to approve its tool calls.

### Activity

Click **Activity** and the sidebar lists what happened to you, newest first, such as **Maya mentioned you in Launch plan** or **Weekly changelog finished a run**. A row you have not seen yet is bright, with a red dot. Click a row to go to the message, the Canvas or the Sidekick it is about; the Activity list stays open beside it, so the next row is one click away. [Activity and notifications](../08-rally-everywhere/03-notifications.md) covers every kind of row.

To make the sidebar narrower or wider, drag its right edge.

## The home pages

The Canvases and Sidekicks sections each have a home page, which shows the whole section at once and fills the window when nothing in the section is open.

The **Canvases** home page has a **New Canvas** button, a search box and filter chips: **Everyone**, **Made by me**, **Shared with me** and **From Sidekicks**. Below them, **Live now** lists the Canvases people have open at this moment, and **Recent** lists the rest. In a new Organization the page shows only **New Canvas** and a **Start from a prompt** card, which is the quickest way in, as you will see on [the next page](05-your-first-canvas.md).

The **Sidekicks** home page has **New Sidekick**, a search box and the filter chips **Everyone**, **Only you**, **Organization** and **Paused**. Each row shows what the Sidekick does, its status, who it is shared with and when it next wakes up.

## An open Canvas

Open a Canvas and it fills the window.

- At the top left floats the Canvas's header pill, with its mark and name. Click it for the Canvas's menu: **Pin**, **Rename…**, **Share…**, **Copy link**, **Copy Canvas ID**, and **Open artifact** once Rally has made something.
- At the top right are the faces of the people who have the Canvas open right now, then **Share**, then a **…** button with the same menu as the pill.
- The conversation fills the middle: your messages, your teammates' messages and Rally's, with the message box at the bottom. It says **Ask Rally or the people here…**.
- Once Rally has made something, it opens in a panel on the right, with its version. Drag the panel's edge to make it wider or narrower.

The whole of [chapter 2](../02-canvases/index.md) is about this screen.

## An open Sidekick

Open a Sidekick and you see its one shared conversation, with a details column beside it. In the conversation, people talk to each other and to the Sidekick, each piece of work the Sidekick does appears as a folded run row, and anything it made appears as a card that opens the Canvas. The details column shows its status, **Pause**, a **…** menu, what it **Wakes on**, what it **Uses**, what it **Writes to**, who it can act as, and its email address if it has one. [Chapter 3](../03-sidekicks/index.md) covers all of it.

## The account menu

Your avatar at the bottom of the rail opens the account menu:

- **Restart to Update** appears at the top when a new version of Rally has downloaded and is ready. See [Keeping Rally up to date](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/04-updates.md).
- **Add new member** invites teammates by email. Only owners and administrators can use it. See [Inviting people](../10-running-an-organization/02-inviting-people.md).
- **Members** opens the list of people in Settings.
- **Sidekicks** opens the Sidekicks home page.
- **Settings** opens the Settings window.
- Below them, how much of this week's shared quota your Organization has left.
- **Log out** signs you out of Rally on this Mac.

If your account can belong to several Organizations, the menu also lists them under **Organizations**, with a check mark beside the one you are in, and any waiting **Invitations**. See [Belonging to several Organizations](../10-running-an-organization/04-several-organizations.md).

For help, **Help → Rally website** in the Mac menu bar opens rallywith.ai, whose **Help Center** link leads to this manual, and [Contacting support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md) tells you how to reach a person.

## Rally remembers where you were

When you quit Rally and open it again, it returns to what you were last looking at, with your sidebar width, the panel you had open and your place in the conversation as you left them. The rail shows the section of the Canvas or Sidekick it reopens; if it reopens on a home page, it keeps the section you last picked. The first time, or if that place has gone, it opens the Canvases home page.

This memory belongs to this Mac; your other devices keep their own.
