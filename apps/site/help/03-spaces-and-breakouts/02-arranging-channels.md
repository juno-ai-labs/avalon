title: Arranging your Threads
description: Pin, sort and archive Threads, Breakouts and Channels so the sidebar shows what matters.

With your Channels in place, the next job is keeping the work you care about
within reach. Most of it happens in the sidebar's **Threads** and **Channels**
lists.

## Sorting the Threads list

The **Threads** list holds every Thread and Breakout you take part in. The label
at the right of the **Threads** heading says how they are sorted. Click it to
move to the next order:

| Order | What comes first |
| --- | --- |
| **Recent** | The Threads with the newest activity. This is where the list starts. |
| **A–Z** | Alphabetical, by name. |
| **Needs attention** | Whatever is counted on the **Threads** button: Threads where you were mentioned, have unread messages or have not looked yet. |

Sorting only changes the order of the main list. Your pins stay at the top in
the order you gave them.

## Pinning

Pinning keeps the things you live in at the top of their list, in a **Pinned**
section, in whatever order you like. You can pin Threads, Breakouts and
Channels.

- **To pin**, right-click a Thread, Breakout or Channel in the sidebar and choose
  **Pin**. From inside, open the Thread's or Channel's menu from its title pill
  and turn on **Pinned in my sidebar** (for a Thread it is under **More**).
- **To unpin**, right-click it and choose **Unpin**, or turn **Pinned in my
  sidebar** off.
- **To reorder your pins**, drag them up and down within **Pinned**. The words
  **Drag to reorder** at the right of the heading are there to remind you.

Pinned Threads and Breakouts sit at the top of the **Threads** list; pinned
Channels sit at the top of the **Channels** list. The **Pinned** section appears
once you have pinned something and goes away again when you unpin the last one.

Pins are yours alone. They follow you to your other devices and change nothing
for anyone else.

If a pin or a new order does not stick, the list stays as it was. Try again in a
moment.

## Renaming

To rename a Thread, click its name in its title pill and choose **Rename**. To
rename a Channel, click its name at the top of its screen and choose **Rename**.
Names can be up to 180 characters.

Renaming, icons and colours are covered in more detail in
[Names, icons and colours](../02-working-in-a-channel/07-name-icon-and-colour.md).

## Archiving

When a piece of work is finished, archive it. Archiving takes the thing out of
everyone's lists and stops anyone from working in it. Rally keeps the content:
there is no button anywhere that deletes a Thread.

| To archive | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A Thread | Click its name in its title pill, choose **More**, then **Archive thread**. |
| A Breakout | Click its name in its title pill, choose **More**, then **Archive breakout**. See [Breakouts](03-breakouts.md). |
| A Sidekick | Click **…** on the Sidekick and choose **Archive Sidekick**. See [The Sidekicks window](../07-sidekicks/04-the-sidekicks-window.md). |

> [!WARNING]
> Archiving a Thread affects everyone, not only you. It leaves the lists of
> everyone who can see it, and nobody, you included, can open it again. Any work
> Rally is doing in it stops, scheduled follow-ups stop, and email sent to the
> Thread's address is refused. Nothing is deleted, but Rally has no way in the
> app to bring an archived Thread or Breakout back. If you only want it out of
> your way, pin the Threads you care about instead, or sort by **Recent** and
> let it sink.

## Making room

To give your work more room, drag the sidebar's right edge to make it narrower.
Rally remembers the width on this Mac. Between two panes, drag the edge between
them to give one more room than the other.
