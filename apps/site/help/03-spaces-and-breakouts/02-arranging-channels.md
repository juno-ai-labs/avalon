title: Arranging your Channels
description: Pin, move, file and archive Channels so the sidebar shows what matters.

With your Spaces in place, the next job is getting the right Channels onto the
right shelves and the busiest ones within reach. Almost all of it happens in the
Channels rail, the column of Channels beside the Spaces rail.

## The Channel menu

Right-click any Channel in the Channels rail for its menu. From top to bottom:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pin** / **Unpin** | Keeps the Channel at the top of this Space's list, for you. See [Pinning](#pinning). |
| **Mark as unread** | Marks the Channel unread again, so it nags you later. |
| **Share** | Invites someone by email into the Channel's main conversation. See [Sharing with people outside your Organization](05-sharing-with-guests.md). |
| **Rename** | Opens **Rename channel**. Names can be up to 180 characters. |
| **Add to space** | Puts the Channel in another Space as well. See [One Channel, several Spaces](#one-channel-several-spaces). |
| **Manage access** | Shows exactly who can read the Channel and why. See [Who can see what](04-who-can-see-what.md). |
| **Remove from space** | Takes the Channel out of the Space you are looking at. |
| **Archive channel** | Retires the Channel for everyone. See [Archiving](#archiving). |

Not every item appears every time. Rally's own built-in Channels do not offer
**Add to space**, **Manage access** or **Remove from space**, and neither does a
Space you only see through one of its Channels.

Renaming, icons and colours are covered in more detail in
[Names, icons and colours](../02-working-in-a-channel/07-name-icon-and-colour.md).

> [!TIP]
> To copy a link to a Channel, use the **…** (**More channel actions**) button
> in the Channel's header and choose **Copy channel link**. It is not in the
> right-click menu.

## Pinning

Channels in the rail are listed alphabetically, which is fair but not always
helpful. Pinning lets you keep the Channels you live in at the top, in a
**Pinned** section, in whatever order you like.

- **To pin**, right-click a Channel and choose **Pin**, or drag it up into the
  pinned area. While you drag, **Drop channel here to pin** shows you where to
  let go.
- **To unpin**, right-click it and choose **Unpin**.
- **To reorder your pins**, drag them up and down. From the keyboard, select a
  pinned Channel, hold <kbd>⌥ Option</kbd> and press <kbd>↑</kbd> or
  <kbd>↓</kbd>. The arrow keys on their own only move the selection.

Pins are yours alone. They follow you to your other devices and change nothing
for anyone else in the Channel. They are also kept per Space, so a Channel that
lives in two Spaces can be pinned in one and not the other. Only pinned Channels
can be reordered; the rest stay alphabetical.

You can pin up to 1,000 Channels, by which point the Pinned section has become
the sidebar and you are back where you started.

The Spaces rail reorders the same way: drag a Space, or select it and use
<kbd>⌥ Option</kbd> with <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd>.

## Moving a Channel to another Space

Drag a Channel from the Channels rail and drop it on a Space in the Spaces rail.
The Space pulses to say it has caught it. The Channel **moves**: it leaves the
Space you dragged it from and lives in the new one instead.

- You can only drop on a Space you are able to change, and not on the Space you
  are already in.
- If the Channel you moved was open, Rally opens the next Channel in the list,
  or the Space's page if it was the last one.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Moving a Channel can change who sees it. Drop it in an Organization-wide Space
> and everyone in the Organization can now read it. Drop it in a private Space
> and it may become hidden from people who used to see it. Read
> [Who can see what](04-who-can-see-what.md) before you rearrange anything
> sensitive.

## One Channel, several Spaces

A Channel can sit in more than one Space at once and shows up under each. That
saves you from copying work that belongs to two teams.

- **To add it to another Space**, right-click the Channel, choose **Add to
  space** and pick the Space. It stays where it was as well. Spaces you cannot
  pick are greyed out with the reason, such as **This channel is already in**
  that Space.
- **To take it out of a Space**, open that Space, right-click the Channel and
  choose **Remove from space**. You can also do this from
  [Manage access](04-who-can-see-what.md).

Every Channel must live in at least one Space. If you try to remove it from its
last one, Rally refuses: **Every channel needs a space, and this would leave one
with none. Add it to another space first.**

Remember that each Space a Channel is in is another group of people who may be
able to read it.

## Archiving

When a piece of work is finished, archive it. Archiving takes the thing out of
the sidebar and stops anyone from working in it. Rally keeps the content: there
is no button anywhere that deletes a Channel.

| To archive | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| A Channel | Right-click it in the Channels rail and choose **Archive channel**. |
| A Breakout | Right-click it in the Channel sidebar and choose **Archive breakout**. See [Breakouts](03-breakouts.md). |
| A Space | Right-click it and choose **Archive space**. See [Spaces](01-spaces.md#archive-and-restore-a-space). |
| A Sidekick | Use **Archive Sidekick** in the Sidekicks window. See [The Sidekicks window](../07-sidekicks/04-the-sidekicks-window.md). |

> [!WARNING]
> Archiving a Channel affects everyone, not only you. It leaves the sidebar for
> everyone who can see it, and nobody, you included, can open it again. Any work
> Rally is doing in it stops, scheduled follow-ups stop, and email sent to the
> Channel's address is refused. Nothing is deleted, but Rally has no way in the
> app to bring an archived Channel or Breakout back. If you only want it out of
> your way, pin the Channels you care about instead.

Spaces are the exception: an archived Space can be restored, as described in
[Spaces](01-spaces.md#archive-and-restore-a-space).

## Making room

The columns of the window can be resized. Hover over the edge of a column until
the hint **Drag to resize** appears, then drag. Double-click the edge, or select
it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, to put the width back how it was. The Spaces rail
and the Channels rail each have a button at the bottom to collapse them
(**Collapse spaces**, **Collapse channels**) and to bring them back.

When the window is too narrow to show everything, Rally collapses the Channels
rail for you, and its button says **Widen the window to expand channels**.

## When something goes wrong

If one of these sidebar changes fails (archiving, pinning, reordering, adding a
Channel to a Space, moving it or removing it), Rally shows a small window in the
middle of the screen. Its title says what failed, such as **Channel could not be
moved** or **Channel could not be pinned**, and one sentence says what state
things are in now, for example that the pinned list is back in the order it was
in. Click **OK** to close it.

Clicking **OK** only says you have seen it. Nothing is retried for you. Most of
these messages suggest trying again in a moment, and that usually works. If it
keeps failing, see [Common problems and fixes](../12-help-and-glossary/02-common-problems.md).
