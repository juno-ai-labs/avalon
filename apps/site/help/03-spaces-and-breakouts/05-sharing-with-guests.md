Sometimes the person you need is not in your Organization: a client reviewing a
proposal, a contractor on one project, an accountant once a year. You can invite
them into a single conversation by email, without giving them anything else.
People who join this way are **guests**.

## Share a conversation

You share from the same window you use to add teammates:

1. Open the Thread or Breakout, click its name in the title pill and choose
   **People**, then click **Change** at the top. The **Add a participant** window
   opens, as described in
   [People in the room](../02-working-in-a-channel/05-people-in-the-room.md).
2. Under the list of members is **Share by email**. Type one or more addresses in
   **Email addresses**. Separate them with spaces, commas or semicolons.
3. Click **Share**.

This shares the conversation you opened the window from: the Thread's main
conversation, or the Breakout.

If an address does not look right, Rally says **Enter valid email addresses,
separated by spaces, commas, or semicolons.** If sharing fails, it says **The
conversation couldn't be shared. Try again.**

### What you are sharing

Sharing gives access to one conversation and nothing around it:

- Sharing a Thread shares the main
  conversation only. Its Breakouts stay private.
- Sharing a Breakout shares that Breakout only. The guest does not get the
  Thread's main conversation, its other Breakouts, or its list of participants.

Inside what you shared, a guest can read and write messages and open the
artifacts and files that belong to that conversation. A guest you shared a
Thread with can also start Breakouts from it, which begin private to them like
anyone else's. They get access to the
conversation itself, not loose copies of the files.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The invitation email quotes the conversation you are sharing: its last five
> messages, each cut to about a hundred words, plus a count of earlier ones. Check
> the address before you click **Share**. A typo sends that text to whoever owns
> the wrong address, and taking the invitation back later cannot unsend an email.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If the address already belongs to someone who uses Rally, they get access the
> moment you click **Share**, without being asked, and join your Organization as
> a guest. Anyone else gets access the first time they sign in with that
> address.

## Add a guest to a Channel

Once someone is a guest, you can bring them into a whole Channel rather than
one conversation at a time. That suits a client who should follow everything on
their project.

1. Open the Channel's menu and choose **Add people**.
2. Pick the guest. They are listed with a **Guest** label.

Only people who are already guests in your Organization are offered. To bring
in someone new, share a conversation with them first, as above.

Inside the Channel, a guest works much as a member does:

- They see the Channel, its feed and its messages, even if the Channel is
  private.
- They open, read and post in every Thread in it that is public to the
  Organization, the same ones a member could. Posting in a Thread makes them
  one of its participants, as it does for a member.
- They start new Threads from the Channel's **Start a thread in** box. A Thread
  they start follows the usual rule: public in a Channel open to the whole
  Organization, private in a private Channel.

They still cannot open a private Thread unless they are one of its
participants, or a Breakout they have not been added to. They cannot rename the
Channel, change its people, or create Channels of their own.

> [!WARNING]
> Adding a guest to **General** lets them read every public Thread in General,
> which is usually most of your Organization's Threads. Make a separate Channel
> for the work you want them to see.

To take a guest out, open **People** in the Channel's menu and choose **Remove
from channel** beside their name. The Channel's public Threads close for them
within moments, except Threads they are a participant of (including any they
started or posted in) and Threads another of their Channels holds.

## What the guest sees

The guest gets an email with a link to the conversation. Signing in with that
email address is all it takes: there is nothing to accept. Only someone who can
sign in as that address gets in, so a forwarded invitation is no use to anyone
else.

Guests use Rally in a web browser, in a lighter version of the app made for
this. They see the conversations shared with them, the Channels they have been
added to with the public Threads in those, and the names of the Channels and
Threads their conversations sit in. They also see who is in your Organization,
by name and picture, so a message shows who wrote it. They see nothing else in
your Organization, and they cannot create Channels or add people.
[Rally in a web browser](../08-rally-everywhere/08-rally-in-a-browser.md) shows
what that looks like from their side.

In the **Members** list in Settings, guests appear with a **Guest** badge.

## Taking access back

People you shared with who have not signed in yet are listed under the share
form as **Guest**. Click the **×** beside one to withdraw their invitation. If
they signed in a moment before, Rally says **They have already joined. Remove
them from the participants instead.**

Rally does not yet have a button in the app for ending the access of a guest who
has joined. If you need that done, write to
[help@rallywith.ai](mailto:help@rallywith.ai) with the conversation and the
guest's email address. [Contacting support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md)
says what else to include. In the meantime, do not post anything in that
conversation that the guest should not read.

However access ends, the same things happen:

- Once access is removed, the guest stops seeing the conversation within moments,
  even if they have it open, and cannot reopen it.
- A withdrawn invitation opens nothing. The person following its link can sign
  in, but the conversation is not there for them.
- Ending access to a shared Breakout leaves anything else that was shared with
  them separately, such as the main conversation, untouched.

## Turning a guest into a member

If a guest becomes a regular collaborator, an Organization owner or
administrator can make them a full member: in the **Members** list in Settings,
click **Make member** beside their name and confirm. See
[Roles: owners, administrators, members and guests](../10-running-an-organization/01-roles.md).
