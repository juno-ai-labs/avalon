---
title: Breakouts
description: Branch a private side conversation off a Channel to explore an idea without derailing the main one.
---

A **Breakout** is a side conversation inside a Channel. You start one when an
idea deserves some room but would hijack the main conversation if you had it
there: a rough first draft, a "what if we tried it the other way", a question you
want to think through before you ask everyone.

Every Channel has its main conversation and as many Breakouts as you like. The
Breakouts are listed in the Channel sidebar, under the main conversation's row, as you saw in
[A tour of the window](../01-getting-started/04-a-tour-of-the-window.md).

## Start a Breakout

There are two ways in.

- **From a message.** Hover over any finished message and click **↳ New
  breakout** underneath it. The Breakout branches from that exact point in the
  conversation.
- **From the Channel sidebar.** Click **New breakout**. The Breakout branches
  from the conversation as it stands now.

Either way, the conversation area turns into a launch screen headed **New
breakout** (or **New breakout from this message**), with a reminder: **Private
to you until you add somebody. Rally brings the conversation so far with it.**

1. In the box labelled **What should this breakout work on?**, write your first
   message, the same way you would in any Channel.
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to create the Breakout. <kbd>⇧ Shift</kbd>
   <kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line instead.

Nothing exists until you send that first message, so changing your mind costs
nothing. Click the **X** (**Cancel new breakout**) to back out, and Rally keeps
what you had typed.

Rally gives the new Breakout a title based on what you asked for. You can rename
it whenever you like, as described below.

Starting a Breakout needs a live connection. While Rally is still connecting,
the launch screen says **Starting a breakout is available once Rally connects**.
If it fails, you will see **The breakout couldn't be created. Try again.**

## What Rally knows in a Breakout

Rally does not start a Breakout from a blank page. It begins with a **summary**
of the main conversation up to the point you branched from, so it already knows
what the Channel is about and what has been decided.

From there:

- Rally in the Breakout can **read** the main conversation when it needs more
  detail than the summary holds.
- It cannot **post** in the main conversation. What happens in the Breakout stays
  in the Breakout until you choose to share it.
- The summary is a summary, not a word-for-word copy. If one exact detail
  matters, say it again in the Breakout.

You cannot choose how much Rally brings with it. It always brings the summary.

## Who can see a Breakout

A new Breakout is **private to you**. Nobody else sees it in the Channel sidebar,
not even the Channel's other participants, until you invite them. It is the one
room in Rally with a door that starts closed.

To bring someone in, click the avatar stack in the Breakout's header (**Manage
breakout participants**) and add them, as described in
[People in the room](../02-working-in-a-channel/05-people-in-the-room.md). The
window reminds you of an important rule: **Added participants can read this
breakout and its shared context. This does not grant access to the parent
channel.**

So adding a colleague to a Breakout lets them read that Breakout, including what
Rally brought into it from the main conversation, but it does not give them the rest of the
Channel. That makes Breakouts a good way to involve someone in one part of the
work without opening up everything.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The summary Rally brings into a Breakout is part of what an added person can
> read. Anything said in the main conversation before you branched may reach them that way, in
> summary form. If the earlier conversation holds something they should not see,
> start a fresh Channel with them instead.

To share a Breakout with someone outside your Organization, see
[Sharing with people outside your Organization](05-sharing-with-guests.md).

## The Breakout menu

Right-click a Breakout in the Channel sidebar for its menu:

- **Rename** gives it a new name.
- **Share** invites people to this Breakout by email.
- **Copy breakout link** copies a link that opens this Breakout. See
  [Search and links](06-search-and-links.md).
- **Archive breakout** retires it.

Right-clicking the main conversation row gives a similar menu for the Channel
itself, with **Make public to organization** at the top, greyed out if the Channel is
already open to everyone. That item makes the whole Channel visible to your
Organization; [Who can see what](04-who-can-see-what.md) explains what that
means and how to undo it.

Hover over a Breakout for a moment to see its full name in a card.

### Archiving a Breakout

**Archive breakout** asks you to confirm, then the Breakout leaves the sidebar
for everyone in it and stops accepting messages. Rally keeps the conversation,
but there is no way in the app to restore it. Archive a Breakout when you are
sure you are finished with it.

## Bring a result back to the Channel

Because a Breakout is private, the documents and files Rally makes there do not
show up in the Channel. When one is worth sharing, attach it:

1. In the Breakout, find the artifact card in the conversation.
2. Right-click it and choose **Attach to channel**.

The artifact now also appears in the Channel, and everyone who can read the main
conversation can open it. The Breakout keeps its own copy of the link, so people
in either place can open it. The private conversation that produced it stays
private.

A few details:

- Only artifacts Rally made in the Breakout can be attached this way.
- If the Channel already has that artifact, the item is greyed out.
- Rally attaches one thing at a time.

Artifacts themselves are covered in [Artifacts](../06-things-rally-makes/01-artifacts.md).

A good Breakout is like a good meeting in a side room: you go in, you work
something out, you come back with the answer, and nobody has to hear you arguing
about the font.
