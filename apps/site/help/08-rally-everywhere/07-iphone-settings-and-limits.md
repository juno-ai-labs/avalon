The iPhone app has a small Settings screen of its own. To open it, go to the
**Channels** tab and tap the round avatar at the top left (VoiceOver calls it
**Organization and settings**). Tap **Done** to close it.

## What is in iPhone Settings

### Appearance

Choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. **System** follows your iPhone. Dark
on the phone is true black, which looks good on the iPhone's screen at night.

This choice is saved on the phone only. Your Mac keeps its own (see [Light, dark
and your own colour](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/02-appearance-and-icon.md)).

### Notifications

**Notify me on this iPhone about mentions and additions** turns alerts on or off
for this phone. [Notifications](04-notifications.md#notifications-on-your-iphone)
explains what you get and when.

### Account

Your **Name**, **Email**, **Organization**, **Region** and **Default timezone**,
for reference. This screen shows them but does not change them.

### Digest

**Send me a digest** turns your [digest](05-the-digest.md) on or off for this
Organization. Below it, **Days** and **Times** show your schedule, but you
cannot edit them here. If you turn the digest on when no schedule has been set,
it starts at weekdays, 9:00 AM.

From the phone, email is the only destination you can save. As the screen puts
it, "Other delivery connectors stay on the Mac." To get your digest in Slack, or
to change days and times, use **Settings → Digest** on the Mac.

### Organization members

A list of the people in your Organization. It is for looking only; inviting and
changing roles happen on the Mac (see [Running an
Organization](../10-running-an-organization/index.md)).

### Connections and This Mac only

These two sections are signposts rather than settings. They remind you that
connecting tools and a handful of Mac features live on the Mac. Tools your
Organization has already connected still work when you chat from the phone.

### Privacy, leaving and signing out

At the bottom are links to the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**,
**Leave this organization**, **Account deletion** and **Sign out**. Leaving asks
you to confirm first. What leaving does is in [Leaving, removing and
closing](../10-running-an-organization/05-leaving-and-removing.md), and what
account deletion does is in [What Rally keeps, and deleting your
data](../11-privacy-and-security/03-what-rally-keeps.md).

## Reading comfortably

The app follows your iPhone's own accessibility settings:

- Text follows the size you set in iOS (**Settings → Display & Brightness →
  Text Size**, or the larger sizes under **Accessibility**).
- It respects **Reduce Motion**.
- VoiceOver reads out the send and stop buttons and the messages.

## What stays on the Mac

The iPhone app is for following and joining in. These all need the Mac app:

| Job | On the Mac, see |
| --- | --- |
| Starting a new Channel | [Your first Channel](../01-getting-started/05-your-first-channel.md) |
| Spaces: creating them, dashboards, pins, managing access | [Spaces](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/01-spaces.md) |
| Creating or editing Sidekicks | [Make a Sidekick](../07-sidekicks/02-make-a-sidekick.md) |
| Connecting tools, GitHub repository access, custom MCP servers, inbound webhooks | [Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md) |
| Where Rally's code may reach on the internet | [Organization settings](../10-running-an-organization/03-organization-settings.md) |
| Mentioning people with **@**, slash commands, reordering queued messages | [The message box](../02-working-in-a-channel/01-the-message-box.md) |
| Opening and previewing files and artifacts, saving replies as images or PDFs, exporting diagrams | [Things Rally makes](../06-things-rally-makes/index.md) |
| Searching the whole Organization | [Search and links](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/06-search-and-links.md) |
| Editing digest days, times and destinations | [The digest](05-the-digest.md) |
| Menu bar, Dock icon, opening at login, Lockdown mode | [Rally on your Mac](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/03-rally-on-your-mac.md) |

If you reach for something on the phone and it is not there, it is almost
certainly on this list, waiting patiently on your desk.
