---
title: The desktop app
description: What the Rally app for macOS and Linux adds to the web app, and how to install it and sign in.
---

Everything in this book so far works in a web browser. Rally also comes as a
desktop app, for Macs with Apple silicon and for Linux. It shows the same
Organizations, Canvases and Sidekicks as the browser, so you can use both and
switch whenever you like. Nothing moves when you do.

You do not need the app. Install it if you want one of the things below.

## What the app adds

- **A built-in browser beside your Canvas.** Rally can open web pages in a
  panel next to the conversation, and you can sign in to sites there. In a
  browser tab, links open in new tabs instead.
- **Reactive sites preview in place.** In the web app you see **Preview
  unavailable here** and a way to open the site in a new tab.
- **Save a reply as an image or a PDF.** The web app has no buttons for this.
- **Windows of its own.** Settings opens in its own window, **Open in a new
  window** opens a real window, and Rally remembers where you put them.
- **Lockdown mode,** which protects the saved sign-in on your computer
  ([Signing in, signing out and Lockdown
  mode](../11-privacy-and-security/01-signing-in-and-out.md#lockdown-mode)).
- **Automatic updates,** described in [Keeping Rally up to
  date](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/04-updates.md).

On a Mac, the app also lives in the menu bar, can open at login, shows counts
on its Dock icon, sends notifications through macOS, and can hear your
computer's sound as well as your microphone. [Rally on your
Mac](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/03-rally-on-your-mac.md) covers those.
Linux has none of these; closing the last window there quits Rally, and your
Sidekicks keep running without it.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (an M-series chip), or a 64-bit Linux computer that
  can install `.deb` packages. Rally is tested on Ubuntu 24.04. There is no
  Windows or Intel Mac version; use the web app there.
- A Google or Apple account to sign in with. The app has no email code
  sign-in, so if you signed up in the browser with a code, sign in with a
  Google or Apple account that uses the same email address.

## Install it

On a Mac:

1. On [rallywith.ai](https://rallywith.ai), click **macOS** under **Also at
   home on your desktop.** The web app's sidebar has a **Download the Mac app**
   button at its foot, which fetches the same file.
2. Open the downloaded **Rally.dmg**.
3. Drag Rally into your **Applications** folder.
4. Open Rally from Applications or Launchpad.

The installer is signed and checked by Apple, so your Mac should open it
without complaint.

On Linux, click **Linux (.deb)** on [rallywith.ai](https://rallywith.ai) and
install the package with your usual package manager.

Older personal download links now lead to the public macOS installer. There is
no token to redeem.

## Sign in

The app opens on **Welcome to Rally**.

1. Click **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Apple**. Rally opens your
   usual web browser at the sign-in page.
2. Choose your account and approve.
3. The browser shows **Returning you to Rally** and hands you back to the app
   after a moment. If it does not, click **Open Rally** on that page, then close
   the tab.
4. Back in the app, the button reads **Completing sign in…** while it finishes.

> [!TIP]
> If the browser says **This sign-in response is incomplete or invalid**, go
> back to the app and click **Continue with Google** again. A fresh start fixes
> it almost every time.

Once you are in, the app looks like the web app: the same rail, sidebar and
Canvases. [A tour of Rally](../01-getting-started/04-a-tour-of-the-window.md)
still applies. The app opens Settings in a window of its own, and
<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> opens it from anywhere on a Mac.
