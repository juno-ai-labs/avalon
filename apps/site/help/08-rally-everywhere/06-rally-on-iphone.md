---
title: Rally on your iPhone
description: "Follow and join your Channels from your phone: read, reply, branch, dictate and chat with Sidekicks."
---

The Rally app for iPhone (it runs on iPad too) puts your Channels in your
pocket. It is a companion to the Mac app rather than a copy, made for following
work and joining in: checking how a job is going, answering a question Rally
asked you, replying to a mention, or sending a quick request while you are away
from your desk. Setting things up stays on the Mac, and the [next
page](07-iphone-settings-and-limits.md#what-stays-on-the-mac) lists exactly what
that covers.

Everything you do on the phone happens in the same Channels your teammates see
on their Macs. Reply from the bus, and the reply is there when they look.

## Getting started

The iPhone app needs iOS 26 or newer. If you would like it and do not have it
yet, ask the Rally team at help@rallywith.ai.

1. Open the app. Three short cards introduce it; tap **Next** through them and
   then **Get started**.
2. Tap **Continue with Google** and sign in with the same Google account you use
   on your Mac. The iPhone app signs in with Google only.
3. If you belong to one Organization, it opens straight away. If you belong to
   several, or have invitations waiting, you see the **Organizations** screen
   first.

### The Organizations screen

- **Your organizations** lists each Organization with its region. Tap one to
  open it.
- **Invitations** shows invitations waiting for you, with who sent them. Accept
  one to join.
- **Create an organization** lets you start a new one. Type a name and tap
  **Create**. The screen also has a **Region** menu. Rally publishes a single
  Global deployment (see [Where your data lives](../11-privacy-and-security/02-what-rally-shares.md#where-your-data-lives)),
  so leave it on **United States** unless the Rally team has told you
  otherwise.

The phone holds one Organization at a time. To get to a different one, come
back to this screen. The guided setup you met on the Mac in [chapter
1](../01-getting-started/03-guided-setup.md) does not run on the iPhone.

## The Channels tab

The first tab lists your Channels, grouped by when you last opened them: **Last
hour**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **This month** and **Before
this month**. A Channel with something new has an unread dot, a preview of the
latest message and its time.

- Pull down to refresh. Scroll to the bottom and older Channels load by
  themselves.
- Swipe a row to the left for **Archive** and **Unread**.
- Press and hold a row for **Rename**, **Make private** or **Make public to
  organization**, **Mark unread** and **Archive**.
- The round avatar at the top left, with your initials and colour, opens
  [Settings](07-iphone-settings-and-limits.md).

The order is by when *you* last looked, not by the latest message. The phone
also has no Spaces, so there is no pinned section and Mac pins do not show
here. And there is no button to start a new Channel: new Channels begin on the
Mac, or in Slack.

## Inside a Channel

Tapping a Channel shows its **Main** conversation, each of your
[Breakouts](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/03-breakouts.md), and a **New breakout**
row. Tap **Main** or a Breakout to open the conversation.

What you see will be familiar from [While Rally
works](../02-working-in-a-channel/03-while-rally-works.md):

- Rally's replies appear as it writes them.
- The steps it took fold into a **Worked** list above the answer.
- When Rally has a question for you, it shows a question card with **Skip** and
  **Submit**.
- If a turn failed, a **Retry** button sits under it.
- An **Unread** divider marks where you stopped reading. In a long conversation
  a down-arrow button, **Jump to latest**, takes you to the bottom. Scroll to the
  top and older messages load by themselves.

The info button at the top right opens **Details**: the Channel's title, its
[email address](03-email-into-rally.md), whether it is public or private, and
the people in it.

Press and hold **Main** or a Breakout in the list for **Rename**, **Copy channel
link** or **Copy breakout link**, and **Archive**. On **Main** you can also
choose **Make public to organization**.

> [!NOTE]
> If your connection drops, the app does not save messages to send later. It
> tells you something went wrong; send again once you have signal and it
> reconnects.

### Starting a Breakout

Under any finished message in **Main**, tap the branch arrow (**New breakout**).
Or tap **New breakout** in the Channel's list. A **New breakout** screen opens,
and your first message creates the Breakout. Until you send that first message,
nothing exists, so backing out costs nothing. When you branch from a message,
that message and the ones before it come along, faded, as context.

You can only branch from **Main**, not from inside another Breakout. A Breakout
is private to whoever started it, and who is in it cannot be changed from the
phone.

## Writing to Rally

The message bar at the bottom says **Ask Rally anything**.

- Type and press <kbd>Return</kbd> to send.
- To attach a photo, tap the paperclip and choose one from your library.
- To attach any other file, press and hold the paperclip and choose **Choose
  file**. This one is easy to miss. Attachments show as chips above the bar,
  each with an × to remove it, and a message with an attachment can be sent
  without any text.
- To talk instead of type, tap the waveform button. A **Speak** sheet opens and shows
  **Listening** while you talk; tap **Send** to send what you said straight away,
  or **Cancel**.
- While Rally is writing, the send button turns into a square. Tap it to stop.

Voice on the iPhone uses Apple's own speech recognition, which needs permission
for both the microphone and speech recognition. If either is turned off, the
sheet says **Microphone is off** or **Speech recognition is off**, and nothing
is sent.

The phone's message bar is simpler than the Mac's. There is no **@** list for
mentioning people and no slash commands. For those, see [The message
box](../02-working-in-a-channel/01-the-message-box.md) on the Mac.

### Ideas for an empty chat

A brand-new, empty chat greets you by name ("Good morning", and so on) and
offers up to six ideas to tap, such as **Catch me up on what's moving**, **Draft
an update for the team** and **What's blocking us?**. Some may be suggestions
Rally has worked out from your connected tools. Tap one and it is sent. These
suggestions appear only on the iPhone.

## Under Rally's replies

Each reply has a row of small buttons:

- **Copy** copies the text. The icon turns into a tick when it has.
- **Speak** reads the reply aloud. Tap it again to stop. Handy when your hands
  are full of shopping.
- **Share** opens the iPhone share sheet with the reply's text.
- The branch arrow starts a **New breakout** from that reply.

Copy and Share give you plain text. Saving a reply as an image or PDF, and
exporting diagrams, are Mac only (see [Saving and sharing what Rally
wrote](../06-things-rally-makes/05-saving-and-exporting.md)).

## Files in the conversation

On the phone, a file in a message shows as a paperclip and its name. The app
cannot open or preview files yet, and artifacts, versions and live site previews
are on the Mac. Photos you send from the phone arrive as JPEG pictures named
`photo.jpg`, whatever they were called in your library.

## The Sidekicks tab

The second tab, with the hexagon icon, lists your Organization's
[Sidekicks](../07-sidekicks/index.md) with their status and description. Tap one
to chat with it. If there are none yet, the tab says "Sidekicks in this
organization will show up here."

The phone is for using Sidekicks, not making them. Creating and editing a
Sidekick happen on the Mac. A Sidekick chat has no Breakouts.

## Opening Rally links on your phone

Tap a link to a Rally Channel or Breakout (one that starts with
`https://rallywith.ai/app/`) and it opens in the iPhone app. To share one, press
and hold a Channel or Breakout and choose **Copy channel link** or **Copy
breakout link**.

Links to things the phone does not have open a page instead. A link to a Space
says "Space dashboards are available in Rally for Mac." and a link to a Sidekick
says "Sidekicks are available in Rally for Mac.", even though the phone has a
Sidekicks tab. If the app is not installed, the page says "If the app does not
open, install Rally first." And if the thing the link points to has gone, the
app tells you the view does not exist or is no longer available.

[When a link won't open](../12-help-and-glossary/01-when-a-link-wont-open.md)
covers the other messages you might see.
