---
title: Rally in a web browser
description: Use Rally in a web browser to join a Canvas someone shared with you, keep up with your Sidekicks, or start an Organization.
---

Rally has a lightweight version that runs in an ordinary web browser, at
**app.rallywith.ai**. It is made for working together in Canvases and Sidekicks
away from the Mac app. A client you invited into a single Canvas, a contractor
helping with one piece of work, a teammate on a computer without the Mac app,
or you on your phone can all open the browser version and join in, with nothing
to install.

It is the light option on purpose, and a few things stay on the Mac (see [the
conversation itself](#the-conversation-itself)). You can start an Organization
there, though. The Canvases and Sidekicks are the same ones everyone else sees.

[Sharing with people outside your Organization](../02-canvases/09-sharing-with-guests.md)
covers the other side of this: how you invite someone, and how you take that
access back.

## Opening something shared with you

When someone shares a Canvas with you, you get an email with a link.

1. Click the link. The browser version asks you to sign in.
2. Sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to (see the next
   section).
3. The shared Canvas opens. There is nothing to accept: signing in with
   that address is all it takes.

- Signing in with a different address opens nothing. The share belongs to the
  address it was sent to, so a forwarded email is no use to anyone else.
- If the invitation has been withdrawn or has expired, signing in shows no new
  Canvas. Ask the person who sent it for a new one.
- If you already use Rally with that address, the shared Canvas is
  already in your list. You get the email so you know it is there.

> [!NOTE]
> An invitation to a shared Canvas is different from an invitation to
> join an Organization. The Organization invitation, at rallywith.ai/invite,
> opens the Mac app instead (see [Create or join an
> Organization](../01-getting-started/02-create-or-join-an-organization.md)).

## Signing in

The browser version signs you in with Google or with your email address, no
password needed. Click **Continue with Google** to use your Google account. To
use email instead:

1. Click **Continue with email**, type your email address and click **Email me a
   sign-in link**.
2. The page changes to **Check your email**. Rally sends you an email titled
   **Sign in to Rally**, containing a code and a link.
3. Either type the code into the **Sign-in code** box and click **Continue**, or
   open the link in the email and click **Continue** on the **Confirm your
   sign-in** page.

The code and the link each work for one sign-in only. If the email does not
arrive, or you typed the wrong address, click **Use another email or request a
new code** to start again.

Once you are signed in you see **Your organizations**, a list of the
Organizations you belong to.

## Starting an Organization

If you do not belong to an Organization yet, ask a teammate to share a Canvas
with you, or start your own: **Your organizations** offers **Create
an organization**. Click it, type a name and click **Create organization**. The new
Organization opens and walks you through the same setup as the Mac app:
how you look to your team, then inviting teammates. Click **Back** to
return without creating one.

### Staying signed in

Your sign-in works across all your tabs and renews itself while you use Rally.
If you leave it alone for a long time you will be asked to sign in again.
**Sign out** is on the first page and **Log out** in the account menu, and signing out in
one tab signs you out in every other tab too, which is what you want on a
shared computer. [Signing in, signing out and Lockdown
mode](../11-privacy-and-security/01-signing-in-and-out.md) has the details.

## Finding your way around

If you have used the Mac app, the layout will look familiar: **Canvases**,
**Sidekicks** and **Activity** down the left edge, with your avatar
(**Settings**) at the bottom, the list beside them, and the open Canvas or
Sidekick filling the rest. [A tour of the window](../01-getting-started/04-a-tour-of-the-window.md)
walks through it. On a phone, the sections sit behind an **Open navigation**
button instead, and the open Canvas takes the whole screen.

- **Canvases** lists the Canvases you can open. A guest with one shared Canvas
  sees little more than that Canvas, which is the point.
- **Sidekicks** lists the Sidekicks shared with you. Guests have none.
- **Activity** lists what happened to you, as in the Mac app; see
  [Activity and notifications](03-notifications.md).
- The account menu under your avatar has the same items as on the Mac,
  including **Settings** and **Log out**.
- On a computer, a **Download the Mac app** button sits at the foot of the
  sidebar. The Mac app is for Macs with Apple silicon. Phones and tablets do
  not show it.

To get notifications in the browser, turn on **Show notifications** under
**Settings → Notifications → In this browser**. On an iPhone or iPad, add Rally
to your Home Screen first (Share, then **Add to Home Screen**), open it from
there, and turn the switch on there. Appearance, which the Mac keeps under
**Application**, is under **Account** in the browser.

Drafts you are typing survive switching to another tab and back.

## The conversation itself

A Canvas works as it does on the Mac: live messages, attachments, pictures in
the lightbox, what Rally made in the panel on the right, comments, and the
people in the Canvas. Everything in [Canvases](../02-canvases/index.md)
applies, with a few exceptions:

- Live sites do not preview inside the page. You see **Preview unavailable
  here** and a way to open the site in your browser instead.
- **Open in a new window** opens an ordinary browser tab or window, and
  artifacts open in a new tab.
- There is no built-in browser panel beside the conversation. Links open in new
  tabs.
- There are no buttons to save a reply as an image or PDF.
- Window positions are not remembered.

## Sharing from the browser

To bring someone else into a Canvas, click **Share** at the top right, as on
the Mac: choose **Specific people** and tick them, or open **Invite a guest by
email** for someone outside your Organization, then click **Save**. Each person
you invite by email gets an invitation like the one you received. Whether you
may change the sharing at all depends on your role; see
[Who can see what](../02-canvases/08-who-can-see-what.md).
