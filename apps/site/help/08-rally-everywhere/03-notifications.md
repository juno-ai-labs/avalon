title: Notifications
description: Choose how mentions, Needs input and finished runs reach you, by push, email or Slack.
---

Rally can tell you about three kinds of thing:

- **Mentions.** Someone @mentions you in a Canvas or in a Sidekick's
  conversation.
- **Needs input.** A Sidekick shared with you is waiting for something only a
  person can give it.
- **Finished runs.** A Sidekick run you care about has finished, such as one you
  asked for.

Everything else, such as new messages or a new version in a Canvas you are in,
shows as a red dot and a count in the sidebar rather than an alert. [Status
dots and badges](../02-canvases/10-status-dots-and-badges.md) explains them.

## Choosing how each one reaches you

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Under **Notify me about**, each kind has a
button for every way Rally can reach you. Click one to turn it on or off; a dark
button is on. Choose any mix, or none.

- **Push** alerts every Mac and browser where you are signed in and have
  notifications on.
- **Email** comes from **Rally <notifications@rallywith.ai>** with an **Open in
  Rally** link.
- **Slack** is a direct message from the Rally app. It appears once Rally is
  [installed in your Slack workspace](01-rally-in-slack.md) and you have linked
  your account with **Connect Rally**.

With none chosen, that kind shows only in the sidebar. Nothing is sent another
way instead.

When the window is narrow, each kind shows one button naming what is on, such
as **Push and Slack**. Click it for a list you can tick.

Your choices apply on every device in that Organization.

Notification text is plain: formatting is removed and mentions appear as
**@Name**.

## Notifications on your Mac

Open **Settings → Notifications**. At the top, under **On this Mac**, one switch
turns alerts on this Mac on or off. It is on unless you turn it off, and your
choices under **Notify me about** still apply to your other devices.

- Alerts arrive even when Rally is closed.
- Click an alert and Rally opens the exact Canvas or Sidekick it is about.
- The first time, macOS asks whether Rally may send notifications. If you said
  no, the settings page says **Notifications are turned off in macOS.** with an
  **Open System Settings** button to change your mind.

> [!NOTE]
> The macOS permission prompt, and a few other system messages, may still say
> "workspace". That is Rally's old word for an Organization.

If you cannot find **Notifications** in Settings at all, notifications have not
been switched on for your account yet. Your teammates will have to keep
mentioning you the old-fashioned way, loudly, across the office.

### The number on the app icon

Rally's icon in the Dock carries a badge: the Canvases with something new for
you plus the Sidekicks waiting for you, the same count as the **Canvases** and
**Sidekicks** buttons on the rail added together. Each Canvas or Sidekick
counts once, however much has happened in it. If you belong to several
Organizations, the badge shows the count for whichever one notified you most
recently, not a total.

## The small print

- Turning the switch off, or refusing permission, takes that device off the
  list. Rally does not send an email instead unless you chose email.
- Signing out of Rally on a Mac stops notifications to that Mac.
- If your device is off or offline, Apple holds alerts for up to 24 hours.
- Now and then the same alert can arrive twice. Rally would rather tell you
  twice than not at all.
- Rally no longer sends a daily digest. The sidebar's counts are the place to
  catch up.
