title: The Rally Manual
description: How to use Rally, from installing it on your Mac to running Sidekicks across your whole Organization.
---

Rally has two halves. In a **Canvas**, you, your teammates and an AI agent talk about a piece of work and the agent makes the thing itself: a document, a spreadsheet, an image, a PDF, a small website. In a **Sidekick**, you hand the agent a job it keeps doing for your team, on a schedule or whenever something happens, and everyone it is shared with can talk to it in one conversation.

This book is laid out the way computer manuals used to be: chapters, in order, each one assuming you have read the one before. Chapter 1 gets Rally installed on your Mac and your first Canvas going. Chapter 2 is about Canvases: the conversation, the things Rally makes in it, and who else can see them. Chapter 3 is about Sidekicks. The middle of the book covers what the agent can do and connecting it to the tools your team already uses. The later chapters are about Slack and notifications, settings, running an Organization, and privacy. The last chapter is for when something goes wrong, and ends with a glossary.

It reads front to back, but nobody will judge you for skipping straight to the chapter you need. Pages point back to whatever they build on, so you can always find your way to the explanation you skipped.

If you have only just downloaded Rally, start with [Getting started](01-getting-started/index.md). If someone shared a Canvas with you and you are using Rally in a web browser, [Rally in a web browser](08-rally-everywhere/04-rally-in-a-browser.md) is the page for you.

Every page of this manual is also published as plain Markdown, and [llms.txt](https://rallywith.ai/help/llms.txt) lists them all, so you can hand the whole book to your own AI tools and ask them questions about Rally. It seemed only fair.

When the book does not have the answer, a person does. Write to [help@rallywith.ai](mailto:help@rallywith.ai) and someone who works on Rally will reply. [Contacting support](12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md) says what to include so the first reply is the useful one.
