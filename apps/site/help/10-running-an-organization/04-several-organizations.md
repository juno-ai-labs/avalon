Plenty of people belong to more than one team, such as their company and a
client who also works in Rally. One Rally account can belong
to several Organizations, and you move between them without signing out.

As the [chapter opening](index.md) said, Organizations are separate worlds. Canvases, Sidekicks, connections and members never cross from
one to another. Rally shows you one Organization at a time.

## Your organizations

In the web app, the first thing you see after signing in is **Your
organizations**: every Organization you belong to, with a count beside any that
has something new for you. Click one to open it. This is the list
[Create or join an Organization](../01-getting-started/02-create-or-join-an-organization.md)
introduced.

## The account menu

Once an Organization is open, click your avatar at the bottom of the rail. The
menu can have an **Organizations** section listing each Organization you
belong to.

- A **check mark** sits beside the one you are in now.
- Another Organization shows a count when something new is waiting for you
  there. Rest your pointer on it to see what.
- If you belong to Organizations in different regions, each row also shows its
  region.

If Rally could not check the other Organizations just now, the menu says **Counts last
updated** and how long ago.

There is deliberately no combined inbox across Organizations. Each one keeps
its own activity.

> [!NOTE]
> Rally is turning on the **Organizations** section gradually, so your account
> may not have it yet. If the account menu has no **Organizations** section,
> it is not on for you. In the web app, **Your organizations** still lists them
> all after you sign in.

## Switch to another Organization

Click its name in the **Organizations** section. A small spinner appears on the
row while Rally gets it ready, then Rally reopens in that Organization, with
its own Canvases, Sidekicks and settings. In the web app the tab reloads into
it.

Now and then the [desktop app](../08-rally-everywhere/04-the-desktop-app.md)
asks you to confirm who you are before it opens another Organization, usually
because you have not been there for a while. The row then says **Finish
signing in to Rally in your browser**, and your browser opens. Sign in there
and Rally carries on. If you closed the browser tab by mistake, click the row
again to reopen it.

If switching does not work, the row tells you why:

- **Connect to open this organization.** You are offline. Rally needs a
  connection to open a different Organization.
- **You are no longer a member of this organization.** Your access has ended,
  and the list refreshes to drop it. See [Leaving, removing and closing](05-leaving-and-removing.md).
- **Rally could not open this organization.** Something else went wrong. Try
  again in a moment, and if it keeps happening, see [Contacting support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md).

## Accept an invitation from a new Organization

When someone in another Organization invites you, the invitation shows up in
two places:

- On **Your organizations**, above the Organizations you already belong to,
  with **Accept** and **Decline**.
- In the account menu, under **Invitations**, as **Join** and the
  Organization's name, with **Invited by** and the person's name underneath.
  In the desktop app, opening the link in the invitation email also pops it up
  by itself.

Click it to open a dialog, then choose **Join**, **Not now** or **Decline**.
The full details, including the email you receive, are in
[Create or join an Organization](../01-getting-started/02-create-or-join-an-organization.md#joining-with-an-invitation).

After you join, the new Organization appears in the **Organizations** list and
under **Your organizations**, ready to switch to.

## Create another Organization

Neither the web app nor the desktop app's account menu offers a way to create a
second Organization today. **Create an organization** appears on **Your
organizations** only while you belong to none, as described in
[Create or join an Organization](../01-getting-started/02-create-or-join-an-organization.md#creating-a-new-organization).
If you need another one, [contact support](../12-help-and-glossary/03-contact-support.md).

Whoever creates an Organization becomes its owner, with everything that comes
with that (see [Roles](01-roles.md)). It starts with nobody in it but you, so the
next stop is [Inviting people](02-inviting-people.md). An Organization of one
is tidy, but the meetings are a bit quiet.
