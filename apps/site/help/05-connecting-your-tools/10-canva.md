Connect your Canva account and Rally can work on your slides and graphics with you. It will not replace a designer, but it is very good at "make three versions of this banner by lunch".

## What Rally can do

Using your own Canva account, Rally can:

- find your designs
- create new designs
- edit designs
- export designs
- read comments on a design and post new ones

Canva decides which of these your Canva plan allows. Some actions may run into a plan limit or use up Canva's generation credits. When that happens, Rally tells you what Canva said.

## Connecting

Canva is personal only, and any member can connect it.

1. Open **Settings → Connections** and find the **Canva** card.
2. Hover over **Connect for me** and read the note. Connecting lets Rally read and change your Canva designs, folders, assets and brand kit. Canva receives a brief description of each thing Rally asks it to do. Watches are off until you turn them on.
3. Click **Connect for me**.
4. Sign in to Canva in your browser and approve access. Keep Rally open until the browser says it worked.
5. Come back to Rally. The card says **Connected**.

If you do not see a Canva card, update Rally to the latest version.

## Watching a design

Connecting does not start watching anything. To have Rally keep an eye on a design, bring it into a Channel and turn on **Watch**, as [Resources: things you bring in](../06-things-rally-makes/04-resources.md) shows. Rally hears about changes once Canva makes them available, and not every single edit is guaranteed to produce a notice.

## Things to try

- "Find the Q3 sales deck in Canva and export it as a PDF."
- "Make a square social post announcing the webinar on the 14th, in our brand colours."
- "Read the comments on the conference banner and summarise what people want changed."

## When it stops working

If Rally asks you to reconnect, or the row in **Settings → Connections** says **Reconnect required**, click **Refresh** and sign in to Canva again. **Delete** the connection to stop Rally using it, along with any watches that depend on it.

If you contact support, send the time and the error you saw. Never send sign-in codes or private Canva share links.
