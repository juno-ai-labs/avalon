title: X
description: Let Rally read public posts, threads, replies and List timelines on X.

The X connector lets Rally read what is public on X (the service once called Twitter) for research and keeping an eye on things. It only reads. It will not post your hot takes for you, however good they are.

## What it is

On **Settings → Connections** the card is called **Public X via TwitterAPI.io**. That name tells you how it works: Rally reads public X content through TwitterAPI.io, an outside service Rally arranges. It does not use your X account, and nobody signs in to X.

With it connected, Rally can read:

- a person's timeline of posts
- the whole thread a post belongs to
- the replies to a post
- the timeline of a public X List

It cannot post, like, follow or send messages, and it cannot see anything private.

## Connecting

X is Organization only, so an owner or administrator sets it up once for everyone.

1. Open **Settings → Connections** and find the **Public X via TwitterAPI.io** card.
2. Click **Connect for organization**. There is no sign-in step.
3. The connection appears in the **Organization connections** table.

The card is offered only in regions where this service is set up. If your Organization's region does not have it, the card is missing or says **Unavailable**. That is not a fault, and there is nothing to fix.

## Things to try

- "What has our company's X account posted this week, and which post got the most replies?"
- "Read the thread from this link and summarise the argument."
- "What are people replying to the launch announcement?"

## When a post is missing

1. Check the account, post or List is public and still on X.
2. Check the **Status** of the X connection in **Settings → Connections**.
3. Give Rally the post's full public link or the exact account name and try again.
