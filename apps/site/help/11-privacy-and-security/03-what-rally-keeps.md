Rally is built to remember. A Channel you started in spring is still there in
autumn, with every message, file and artifact version. That is the point of
it, and it also means you should know how long things stay and how to have
them removed. This page summarizes section 8 of the
[Privacy Policy](https://rallywith.ai/trust/privacy/) (effective September 21,
2026) and explains what the buttons in the app do.

> [!NOTE]
> The Privacy Policy still uses some of Rally's old names. Where it says
> *workspace*, read Organization; *work streams* are Channels; *automations*
> are Sidekicks.

## How long Rally keeps things

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your work: Channels, Breakouts, messages, files, artifacts, Sidekicks and their runs, including anything Rally fetched from your connected tools while doing the work | No automatic expiry. It stays until someone with the right to do so deletes it, or the Organization is deleted. |
| The Organization's audit records (who joined, who changed what) | For as long as the Organization exists. |
| The one-time check Rally uses when you link your Slack account | 15 minutes. |
| Rally's own records of product events and deliveries | Up to 90 days. |
| Reduced analytics summaries | Up to 13 months. |
| Usage reports waiting on your Mac to be sent | At most 14 days. If they have not gone by then, they are not sent. |
| Account, security and legal records | As long as reasonably needed for those purposes. |

Two more points from the policy:

- Backups. When something is deleted from Rally's live systems, a copy can
  remain in encrypted backups for a while. Backups are kept apart and expire on
  a normal rotation.
- Anonymous figures. Rally may keep information that has been stripped of
  anything that could reasonably identify a person.

## Archiving is not deleting

Throughout this manual you have met **Archive**: for Channels and Breakouts in
[Arranging your Channels](../03-spaces-and-breakouts/02-arranging-channels.md),
for Spaces, for Sidekicks and for resources. In every case archiving takes
something out of sight. It does not erase it.

- A Channel. Archiving takes it out of the sidebar for everyone who can see
  it, and nobody, you included, can open it again. Any run in progress stops,
  scheduled follow-ups stop, and mail to its address is refused. As the dialog
  says: **Nothing is deleted, but Rally has no way to bring a channel back.**
- A Breakout. The same: it is kept, and there is no control in the app to
  restore it.
- A Space. Archiving removes it from the Spaces rail. Spaces are the one
  thing you can restore from the app.
- A Sidekick. Its schedules stop, and its conversation and run history stay
  stored but can no longer be viewed. It cannot be unarchived.
- A resource. It leaves the Channel's list. The original file in Google
  Drive, Slack or wherever it came from is not touched.

Archive when you want something gone from view. If you need the content
erased, ask for deletion as described below.

## Disconnecting a tool

When you delete a connection in
[Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md), Rally
stops reaching that tool. Work Rally already did with it stays in your
Channels. The Privacy Policy spells this out for Slack: disconnecting stops new
access but does not delete Rally Channels, summaries or artifacts that already
contain Slack information. To remove the Rally app from Slack itself, a Slack
administrator also has to uninstall it in Slack.

## Leaving an Organization, and closing one

Leaving an Organization removes it from your Rally account on every device. The
work you did there stays with the members who remain.

If you are the last member, leaving closes the Organization: nobody can open it
again. Closing makes it unavailable **without deleting its retained data**. To
have that data deleted, ask for it as described below.

[Leaving, removing and closing](../10-running-an-organization/05-leaving-and-removing.md)
walks through both.

## On your iPhone

The iPhone app's **Settings** has an **Account deletion** section with two
items. Their names promise a little more than one of them does, so read this
before you tap.

**Delete account on this device** asks you to confirm in a box titled
**Delete account on this device?**, then:

- leaves the Organization you have open on the phone,
- signs you out, and
- removes your saved sign-in from the iPhone's Keychain.

It does **not** erase your Rally account, and it does not erase the
Organization's data for the other members. The screen says so, and so do we.

**Request data deletion** opens Rally's Privacy Policy, which explains how to
ask for real erasure.

> [!WARNING]
> **Delete account on this device** makes you leave your current Organization,
> which affects every device you use, not only the phone. To come back you need
> a new invitation. If you only want the phone signed out, tap **Sign out**
> instead.

## Asking for your data to be deleted

There is no button in the app that erases your account or an Organization's
data. To ask for deletion, email help@rallywith.ai. Say what you want
deleted and which Organization it belongs to. Do not send passwords or the
content itself.

The same address handles the other rights you may have, depending on where you
live: to see, correct, export or restrict what Rally holds about you.

What happens next:

- Rally checks who you are before acting, and for content inside an
  Organization, that you have the authority to ask.
- Content belongs to the Organization. Rally handles an Organization's work
  on its behalf, so a request about that work usually goes to the people who run
  your Organization first. If you are not an owner or administrator, talk to one.
- Some records may be kept where the law allows or requires it, such as
  security and legal records.

Chapter 12 has more on writing to the support team, including what to put in
your message.
