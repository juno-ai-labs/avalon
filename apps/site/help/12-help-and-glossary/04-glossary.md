---
title: Glossary
description: Every Rally word in one alphabetical list, including the old names you may still see.
---

Every word Rally uses for something of its own, in alphabetical order. Each
entry is short and points to the page that explains it properly. Old names
are listed too, with an arrow to the name Rally uses today, so if a button or
message uses a word you cannot find anywhere else in this manual, look here.

## A

**Administrator.** An Organization role. Administrators manage members,
invitations, Organization settings and the Organization's connections. Where
this manual says "an owner or administrator", either can do it. See
[Roles: owners, administrators, members and guests](../10-running-an-organization/01-roles.md).

**Archive.** Taking a Sidekick or a resource out of sight while keeping what it
holds. Archiving is not deleting. See
[What Rally keeps](../11-privacy-and-security/03-what-rally-keeps.md#archiving-is-not-deleting).

**Artifact.** A file Rally made for you in a Canvas: a document, a
spreadsheet, a picture, a PDF, a voice recording, a zip archive or a live site.
It shows in the panel on the right of the Canvas and keeps every version. See
[Artifacts](../06-things-rally-makes/01-artifacts.md).

**Attachment.** A file you add to a message you send. See
[Attaching files](../02-canvases/03-attaching-files.md).

**Automation.** The old name for a [Sidekick](#s). A few labels and messages
still say it.

## B

**Background agent.** See [Helper](#h).

**Breakout.** A side conversation inside a Thread, before Rally moved to
Canvases. Breakouts were removed with Threads. See [Canvas](#c).

## C

**Canvas.** One piece of work: a conversation with Rally and the people you
share it with, and the things Rally makes there, shown in the panel on the
right. Private to its creator until shared. See
[What a Canvas is](../02-canvases/01-what-a-canvas-is.md).

**Canvases home page.** The page that shows every Canvas you can open, with
**New Canvas**, search, the filter chips and the **Live now** group. See
[What a Canvas is](../02-canvases/01-what-a-canvas-is.md#the-home-page).

**Channel.** A list of Threads, before Rally moved to Canvases. Channels were
removed, and their links no longer open. See [Canvas](#c).

**Comment.** A note you pin to an exact part of what Rally made, a word, a
cell, an area of an image or an element of a site, and send to the
conversation. See [Comments](../02-canvases/06-comments.md).

**Comment mode.** The button in the panel's header that lets you pick a part
of what Rally made to comment on. See [Comments](../02-canvases/06-comments.md).

**Connection.** One link between Rally and another tool, such as your Gmail or
your team's Linear. Each connection is either personal or the Organization's.
See [Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md).

**Connector.** A kind of tool Rally can connect to: Google, GitHub, Slack and
so on. Each one has a card in **Settings → Connections**, and you connect it
to make a connection. See
[Connecting your other tools](../05-connecting-your-tools/index.md).

**Continue.** The only button in the **Rally could not open that link**
window. It closes the window and does not try the link again. See
[When a link won't open](01-when-a-link-wont-open.md).

**Custom tool.** A small program a Sidekick's drafting assistant writes and
tests, saved with the Sidekick so every run can use it. See
[Tools and delegation](../03-sidekicks/06-tools-and-delegation.md#custom-tools).

## D

**Deep link.** A link that opens the Rally app on your Mac at a particular
place: an invitation, the end of signing in, the end of connecting a tool, a
Canvas or a Sidekick. When Rally refuses one, it tells you why. See
[When a link won't open](01-when-a-link-wont-open.md).

**Delegation allowed.** The list of people a Sidekick may act as, using their
current access, when its triggers start a run or one of them asks it for work.
You add and remove yourself. See
[Tools and delegation](../03-sidekicks/06-tools-and-delegation.md#delegation-allowed).

**Drafting assistant.** The part of a Sidekick that writes and changes its
name, instructions and triggers while its managers describe what they want.
See [Make a Sidekick](../03-sidekicks/02-make-a-sidekick.md).

## E

**Edit Sidekick.** The mark a manager puts on a message in a Sidekick's
conversation to change the Sidekick instead of asking it for work. A marked
message goes to the drafting assistant; any other message addressed to the
Sidekick starts a run. See
[Talking to a Sidekick](../03-sidekicks/03-the-shared-conversation.md#managers-change-the-sidekick-here-too).

## G

**Guest.** Someone outside your Organization who has been invited into a
Canvas by email. A guest has no role in the Organization and sees only the
Canvases shared with them, plus who is in the Organization. See
[Sharing with people outside your Organization](../02-canvases/09-sharing-with-guests.md).

## H

**Header pill.** The rounded name at the top of an open Canvas. Click it to
rename the Canvas, copy its link and reach its other settings. See
[A tour of the window](../01-getting-started/04-a-tour-of-the-window.md#an-open-canvas).

**Helper.** An extra agent Rally starts to do part of a big job side by side
with the rest. A line above the message box counts the ones running, as
background agents. See
[Helpers, other conversations and receipts](../04-working-with-rally/06-helpers-and-receipts.md).

## L

**Live now.** The group on the Canvases home page listing the Canvases people
have open at this moment. See
[People in a Canvas](../02-canvases/07-people-in-a-canvas.md#who-is-here-now).

**Live site.** A web page or small app Rally builds and publishes at its own
public address. Its card in the conversation is labelled **Site**. See
[Live sites](../06-things-rally-makes/03-live-sites.md).

**Lockdown mode.** A setting on the Mac that protects your saved sign-in with
your Mac's own keychain. See
[Signing in, signing out and Lockdown mode](../11-privacy-and-security/01-signing-in-and-out.md).

## M

**Manager.** Someone who may change a Sidekick: its author, and the
Organization's owners and administrators. See
[What a Sidekick is](../03-sidekicks/01-what-a-sidekick-is.md#who-a-sidekick-is-shared-with).

**Member.** The everyday Organization role. See
[Roles: owners, administrators, members and guests](../10-running-an-organization/01-roles.md).

**Memory.** Facts Rally keeps between conversations, each one a single
sentence, so you only have to tell it something once. A memory belongs to a
Canvas, a Sidekick, you or the whole Organization, and that decides who it
helps. See
[Memory and coming back later](../04-working-with-rally/05-memory-and-coming-back-later.md).

## N

**Needs input.** A Sidekick, or a run, waiting for information only a person
can give it. Sidekicks that need input sit in their own group at the top of
the Sidekicks sidebar. It never means waiting for permission to use a tool.
See [Talking to a Sidekick](../03-sidekicks/03-the-shared-conversation.md#when-the-sidekick-needs-input).

## O

**Organization.** Your team's shared home in Rally. Everything else (Canvases,
Sidekicks, connections) lives inside one. Old name: Workspace, which still
turns up in a few places, including some macOS permission messages. See
[Create or join an Organization](../01-getting-started/02-create-or-join-an-organization.md).

**Organization connection.** A connection made with **Connect for
organization**. Everyone in the Organization can use it through Rally. Only an
owner or administrator can set one up or fix it. Compare
[personal connection](#p). See
[Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md).

**Origin.** What started a Sidekick run from outside Rally, such as the email
it was sent. Rally attaches it to the run. See
[Resources: things you bring in](../06-things-rally-makes/04-resources.md).

**Owner.** The person who created the Organization. Owners can do everything
an administrator can. See
[Roles: owners, administrators, members and guests](../10-running-an-organization/01-roles.md).

## P

**Participant.** Someone added to a Canvas, who can open it whatever its other
settings. See [People in a Canvas](../02-canvases/07-people-in-a-canvas.md).

**Personal connection.** A connection made with **Connect for me**. It is
private to you and acts as your own account in the other tool. It is separate
from any Organization connection to the same tool. See
[Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md).

**Pinned.** The group at the top of the Canvases and Sidekicks lists that holds
what you pinned, in the order you drag it into, for you alone. See
[What a Canvas is](../02-canvases/01-what-a-canvas-is.md#the-canvases-list).

**Plugin.** A bundle of related abilities Rally loads when a request needs
it, such as the Web, the Terminal, or one for each connected tool. Loading a
plugin never gives Rally more permission than it already had. See
[How Rally works](../04-working-with-rally/01-how-rally-works.md).

**Project.** An old name for what later became a Channel. See [Canvas](#c).

**Public link.** The web address of a live site. Anyone who has it can view
the latest version, so share it with care. See
[Live sites](../06-things-rally-makes/03-live-sites.md).

## R

**Rail.** The narrow strip down the left edge of the window, with
**Canvases** and **Sidekicks** at the top and **Settings** at the bottom. See
[A tour of the window](../01-getting-started/04-a-tour-of-the-window.md#the-rail).

**Rally.** The app, and also the name of the AI agent that works inside it.
"Open Rally" means the app; "ask Rally" means the agent. See
[How Rally works](../04-working-with-rally/01-how-rally-works.md).

**Receipt.** The record Rally keeps of every action that changes something in
another tool, so that a retried run never does the same thing twice. See
[Helpers, other conversations and receipts](../04-working-with-rally/06-helpers-and-receipts.md).

**Reconnect required.** A connection's status when the other tool stopped
accepting Rally's saved sign-in. Fix it with **Refresh**. See
[Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md).

**Refresh.** On a connection, signing in to the other tool again to renew it.
It does not fetch new data. See
[Settings → Connections](../05-connecting-your-tools/01-connections.md).

**Request ID.** The reference shown on a **Run failed** card. Send it to
support so they can find that exact run. See
[Contacting support](03-contact-support.md).

**Resource.** Anything a Canvas holds that came from outside: an uploaded
file, a document from a connected tool, an email. Things Rally made are
artifacts instead. See
[Resources: things you bring in](../06-things-rally-makes/04-resources.md).

**Roles.** The standing a person has in an Organization: owner, administrator
or member. Guests have no role. See
[Roles: owners, administrators, members and guests](../10-running-an-organization/01-roles.md).

**Run.** One stretch of Rally working on a request, from start until it
answers, is stopped or fails. A Sidekick's run is one time it does its job,
and shows as a row in its conversation. See
[While Rally works](../02-canvases/04-while-rally-works.md) and
[Talking to a Sidekick](../03-sidekicks/03-the-shared-conversation.md#run-rows).

## S

**Share.** The button at the top right of a Canvas, for adding people, inviting
guests by email and opening the Canvas to the whole Organization. See
[Who can see what](../02-canvases/08-who-can-see-what.md).

**Sidekick.** An AI agent your team shares. You describe its job once, and it
runs on a schedule, when something happens in another tool, by email or
webhook, or whenever someone asks it in its conversation. Old name:
Automation. See
[What a Sidekick is](../03-sidekicks/01-what-a-sidekick-is.md).

**Sidekick conversation.** The one conversation each Sidekick has, shared by
everyone it is shared with, holding people's messages, run rows and Canvas
cards. See [Talking to a Sidekick](../03-sidekicks/03-the-shared-conversation.md).

**Skill.** A short guide Rally reads before a tricky kind of job, so it does
it properly. A skill adds know-how, never permissions. See
[How Rally works](../04-working-with-rally/01-how-rally-works.md).

**Space.** An old name for what later became a Channel. See [Canvas](#c).

**Status dot.** The coloured dot and label showing the state of a run:
**Running**, **Needs input**, **Latest run completed**, **Latest run stopped**
or **Needs intervention**. See
[Status dots and badges](../02-canvases/10-status-dots-and-badges.md).

**Steering.** Sending a message while Rally is in the middle of a run, so it
changes course without starting again. Your message shows **Unseen** until
Rally has read it. See
[While Rally works](../02-canvases/04-while-rally-works.md).

**Substream.** An old name for what later became a Breakout. See [Canvas](#c).

## T

**Terminal.** Rally's own private Linux computer, where it runs code, works on
data and builds files. Your Organization decides how far it may reach on the
internet. See
[Code, data and the Terminal](../04-working-with-rally/03-code-files-and-the-terminal.md).

**Thread.** A conversation about one piece of work, before Rally moved to
Canvases. Threads were removed, and their links no longer open. The work they
held now happens in a [Canvas](#c).

**Trigger.** What starts a Sidekick: a time, an interval, an event in another
tool, a webhook, an email, or starting it by hand. See
[Triggers and schedules](../03-sidekicks/04-triggers-and-schedules.md).

## U

**Update channel.** How quickly new versions of the Mac app reach you:
**Stable** (at most one release a day, the default) or **Faster** (every
release). See [Keeping Rally up to date](../09-settings-and-shortcuts/04-updates.md).

## V

**Version.** A saved earlier copy of an artifact. Rally publishes a new
version each time it changes one, keeps every version and numbers them, so
**version 3** is the third. See
[Artifacts](../06-things-rally-makes/01-artifacts.md#versions).

## W

**Wake-up.** A reminder Rally sets itself to come back to a Canvas later and
carry on with a job. See
[Memory and coming back later](../04-working-with-rally/05-memory-and-coming-back-later.md).

**Watch.** A switch, still to come in the Mac app, that will tell Rally to
notice when a resource's original changes. See
[Resources: things you bring in](../06-things-rally-makes/04-resources.md#bringing-things-in-yourself).

**Work stream.** An old name for what later became a Thread. See [Canvas](#c).

**Workspace.** The old name for an [Organization](#o). Slack also has
workspaces of its own, so this manual says "Slack workspace" when it means
Slack's.
