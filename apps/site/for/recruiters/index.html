Before you connect

Candidate mail is personal.

So here is exactly what Rally does with it. It reads your mail only after you connect Gmail yourself, and you can remove the connection at any time. A page like the ones above only needs to read. Rally changes nothing in your mailbox unless you ask it to, such as writing a draft or sending a reply. It has no way to delete mail for good.
