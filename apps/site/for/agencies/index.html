Nothing new to keep up

You didn’t go freelance to maintain a project tool

Rally sits on top of the Gmail you already have. It adds one window and changes nothing else.

  • Clients keep emailing

    They write “quick question” the way they always have. They never see Rally, sign up for anything or fill in a form.

  • You keep replying from Gmail

    No cards to drag, no statuses to set, no tickets to close. Rally works out who owes whom from the mail itself.

  • The wall keeps itself

    It updates when mail arrives. Nobody has to remember to update it, which is lucky, because nobody would.

How to start

Three steps. About five minutes.

Nothing to install and nothing to set up. You can do it between two emails.

  1. 1

    Connect your Gmail

    Sign up for free and connect your Gmail. Google asks you to approve it once. Rally works with Gmail today, not Outlook.

  2. 2

    Say what you want to see

    Type one sentence, the way you’d say it to a colleague. Any of these will do:

    • “Make me a wall of my clients. Who’s waiting on me, and for how long?”
    • “List everything Halden Dental has asked for since we signed.”
    • “Which proofs are out for sign-off, and since when?”

    Rally reads the mail and builds the page.

  3. 3

    Leave it alone

    From then on, every client email updates the page on its own. You never refresh it or fill it in. Want it different? Say “put retainer clients first” and Rally changes it. Want a nudge? Ask it to email you each morning with who has waited longest.

Before you connect

It’s your clients’ mail too

So here, plainly, is what Rally can and can’t do with it.

  • A wall like this only needs to read your mail. Rally reads it only after you connect Gmail yourself, and you can remove the connection at any time.
  • Rally changes nothing in your mailbox unless you ask it to. Ask, and it can write a draft, send a reply, or label and archive mail.
  • It has no way to delete mail for good.
  • In a studio, each person connects their own inbox. Nobody’s mail is read until they allow it.
  • You choose who sees a page: the teammates you pick, or the whole studio. They see the page, not your inbox.
  • Rally has completed an independent security assessment. See our security controls
