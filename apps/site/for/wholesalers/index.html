The quote you forgot

A quote with no answer is a sale going cold.

You quoted on the 10th. They said they’d get back to you. Then the week happened. Rally lists every quote you have out, oldest first, with the days since. Anything past five days sits at the top, where you can’t miss it.

When a customer answers, their row changes on its own. You don’t tick anything off.

What it replaces

Nothing new to keep up.

  • The spreadsheet you update at 9pm
  • The sticky notes around the monitor
  • Searching the inbox while the customer holds

A board you have to feed is one more job, and it is wrong by Wednesday. This one feeds itself. You email. Your customers email. Your suppliers email. Rally reads it and keeps the pages current.

Nobody logs an order, tags a thread or moves a card. Your customers and suppliers see nothing different. You keep quoting, invoicing and shipping exactly the way you do now. Rally adds one window on top.

How to start

Connect. Ask. Leave it alone.

  1. 1

    Connect your Gmail

    Sign up free. Google asks you to approve it once. Nothing to install and no card to enter.

  2. 2

    Say what you want to see

    One sentence, in your own words. Rally reads the mail and builds the page in about five minutes. Sellers type things like:

    • “Put every order in my inbox on a board: new, quoted, confirmed, shipped.”
    • “List every quote I sent that nobody answered, oldest first.”
    • “Show what we’re waiting on from suppliers this week, from my inbox and Jay’s.”
  3. 3

    Leave it alone

    From then on, every new email updates the page. You never refresh it and you never fill it in. Want backorders in their own column, or standing orders on their own list? Say so, and Rally changes it.

Before you connect

Your inbox stays yours.

Rally sees your mail only after you connect Gmail yourself, and you can disconnect it any time. A page like the ones above only needs to read your mail. Rally changes nothing in your mailbox unless you ask it to. It has completed an independent security assessment.

One limit worth knowing: Rally reads the email, not the PDF attached to it. If the PO number is in the subject or the message, it is on the board.
