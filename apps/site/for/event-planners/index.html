Your first five minutes

How to start, as a run of show.

Nothing to install and nothing to set up. You can do it between two vendor calls.

  1. 0:00

    Connect your Gmail

    Sign up for free and connect your Gmail. Google asks you to approve it once.

  2. 0:01

    Say what you want to see

    Type one sentence, in your own words. A planner might type:

    • “Make a page for the Lee and Park wedding on June 14: every vendor and where each one stands.”
    • “Show all my events, nearest date first, with what is still open on each.”
    • “List every deposit and final payment a vendor has asked me for, by due date.”

  3. 0:05

    Your page is there, and it stays current

    Rally reads the mail and builds the page. From then on, every time a vendor or a client emails, the page updates itself. You never refresh it or fill it in. Want it to look different? Say so, and Rally changes it.

Before you connect

A plain note on your email.

Rally reads your mail only after you connect Gmail yourself, and you can remove the connection whenever you like. A page like the ones above only needs to read your mail. Rally changes nothing in your mailbox unless you ask it to, such as drafting a confirmation to a vendor. It has no way to delete mail for good.

It works from what the emails say. It does not read inside attached contracts or invoices, take payments or sign anything, so a deposit counts as paid when an email says so. Rally has completed an independent security assessment.
