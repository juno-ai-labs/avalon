Slack troubleshooting

Open Avalon → Settings → Connections to test, reconnect, or disconnect a Slack installation. Open Avalon’s Home tab in Slack to link your Slack identity, start a task, and manage notifications.

Include the approximate time, your Avalon region, and any visible request ID. Never send Slack tokens, OAuth codes, link tokens, passwords, or private message contents by email.
