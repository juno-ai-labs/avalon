Canva.

Use your own Canva account to find, create, edit and export designs in Rally.

Connect your account

  1. 1

    Open Settings → Connections → Canva in Rally.

  2. 2

    Choose the personal connection and review the access disclosure. Canva tools can change designs, post comments and consume generation credits.

  3. 3

    Sign in to your own Canva account in the browser and approve access. Keep Rally open until the browser confirms the connection.

Availability

Canva is available to every Rally member. If you do not see it, update Rally to the latest version.

Using designs

Ask Rally to work with a design you can access. Canva decides which tools your account can use and may return a plan restriction, trial allowance or credit usage. Rally reports that result.

Connecting does not start watching your designs. Watches require selecting a design and explicitly enabling observation. A watch reports changes after Canva makes them visible; it does not guarantee a notification for every edit.

Reconnect or disconnect

If Rally asks you to reconnect, return to Settings → Connections → Canva and sign in again. Disconnect there to stop Rally using that connection and its watches.

Rally stores provider credentials encrypted on its backend. The desktop browser callback carries a short-lived authorization code. Each Canva operation includes a short description of the requested operation.

Get help

Send the time of the failure and the error shown to Rally support. Do not send authorization codes, credentials or private Canva share links.
