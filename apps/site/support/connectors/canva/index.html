Canva.

Use your personal Canva account through live hosted tools to find, generate, edit, comment on, resize, or export designs.

Connect Canva

Open Rally → Settings → Connections → Canva, choose Connect for me, and complete Canva’s browser authorization. Canva connections are personal: your authorization and designs are available only when Rally acts for you.

The tools available in Rally are discovered live from Canva, so their exact names and capabilities can change when Canva updates its service.

If Canva needs attention

  1. 1

    Check the Canva connection’s Status column in Rally.

  2. 2

    If the status says Reconnect required, choose Refresh and complete Canva’s authorization again.

  3. 3

    Confirm the Canva account you authorized can still open the design, folder, asset, or brand kit you asked Rally to use.

Support details

Send the approximate time, your Rally region, and any visible request ID. Never send an access token or a Canva URL that contains access material.
