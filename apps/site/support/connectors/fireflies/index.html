Fireflies.

Your own Fireflies meetings through its live tools. Fireflies connects to your account, not to your whole workspace.

Connect for me

In Rally Settings → Connections → Fireflies, choose Connect for me and complete Fireflies' browser sign-in. Your work streams then gain the tools Fireflies' own MCP server publishes, against your Fireflies account and nobody else's.

That catalogue is Fireflies', not Rally's, and it includes actions that change and share meetings. Every call is recorded before it runs, and these tools are excluded from sub-agent runs. Rally does not ask you to confirm individual tool calls.

Fireflies' sign-in grants Rally the profile and email scopes it publishes and nothing more. If Fireflies issues no long-lived refresh, your connection asks you to sign in again when its access expires; Settings shows it as needing reconnection.

Team meetings

Rally no longer connects a whole Fireflies workspace. One shared API key reads whatever the account it belongs to can reach — which for a meeting recorder is other people’s calls — and Fireflies publishes no way to say what a key covers, so nobody pasting one could be told what they were sharing. Each person connects their own Fireflies account instead.

A workspace connection made before this keeps working, and meetings already reaching Rally keep arriving. Removing it in Settings → Connections is the one change left to make to it, and it cannot be replaced.

Check and recover

Refresh your personal connection in Settings → Connections to sign in again; Rally marks it as needing reconnection when Fireflies stops accepting it. Use Get help if a problem continues.

Removing a connection stops Rally reading and receiving immediately. Where a workspace connection had a webhook configured in Fireflies, Rally cannot delete it for you: delete it there, or leave it — deliveries to a removed connection are rejected.

For support, share the connection ID, region, error, and affected time. Never share API keys, signing secrets, OAuth tokens, or meeting content.
