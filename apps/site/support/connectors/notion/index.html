Notion.

Use your personal Notion through live hosted tools, or share selected pages with Rally as a workspace connection.

Choose a connection mode

Connect only for me opens Notion’s browser authorization for your personal tools. Connect workspace lets a Rally workspace owner or administrator authorize the Rally integration and choose which Notion pages it can reach.

Only pages shared during workspace installation are visible to that connection. If content is missing, check page sharing before reconnecting.

If Notion needs attention

  1. 1

    Check the same personal or workspace connection’s Status column in Rally.

  2. 2

    Confirm the target page is still shared with the Rally integration in Notion.

  3. 3

    If the status says Reconnect required, ask a workspace owner or administrator to use Refresh.

Page visibility

Notion workspace access is selected, not workspace-wide by default. A page outside the shared selection will remain unavailable even when the connection is healthy.
