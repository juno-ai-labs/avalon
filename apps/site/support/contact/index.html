What to include

Safe details to send

The connector or feature, approximate time, your Rally region, what you expected, what happened, and any visible request ID. Screenshots are useful after private content is removed.

Never send

Passwords, API keys, Slack tokens, OAuth codes, sign-in links, invitation tokens, private messages, or full customer documents.

Security and privacy

For a suspected vulnerability, use the reporting guidance in the Security page. For a privacy request, use the contact process in the Privacy Policy.
