If you already have both Claude Team and Slack, Claude Tag seems a perfect fit. In fact, we believe it's a great fit for software development startups since Claude Tag was an internal project made public. Rally becomes the stronger product when real-time multi-human collaboration is how you want to work as a team.

## Side by side

| | Rally | Claude Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Built for | Teams who work with one shared AI | Claude Team and Enterprise customers who work in Slack |
| People in one AI conversation | Yes. Everyone in a Channel talks to the same Rally. | Yes. One Claude per Slack channel that anyone there can tag. |
| Shared context | Spaces and Channels keep files, decisions, and history for people and AI alike. | Slack threads, plus memory Claude keeps per channel and workspace. |
| Private side work | Breakouts for one-on-one or small-group work, merged back when ready. | Direct messages, which run on your own Claude seat. |
| Your tools | Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Granola, and more. Reads and writes back. | Connections, plugins, and skills an admin sets up per channel or workspace. |
| Runs on its own | Sidekicks run on a schedule or when something happens. | Yes. Scheduled jobs, channel watching, and triggers. |
| Models | One voice, with each job routed to the model family best at it. | Claude. |
| Pricing | $100 or $250 per organization a month. No seat limit. | Needs a Team or Enterprise plan. Channel work is billed by usage, under a spend limit. |

## Where Rally wins

### A bill you can say out loud

Rally is $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with one shared quota and no seat limit. Your finance person can put it in the budget once and move on.

### The work has a home

A Space holds the files, decisions, and history for a project, and Channels keep each conversation in its place. Rally works from that history, and the drafts and dashboards it makes are saved right there in the Channel.

### No setup project

Nobody writes system prompts, skills files, or plugins. When a job needs a specialty, like your brand voice or a follow-up after every sales call, Rally creates a Sidekick for itself and keeps using it.

### It knows who can see what

Rally keeps track of who asked what and who is allowed to see what, across people and connectors. A shared AI stays shared without oversharing, and Breakouts let you rough something out before the whole team sees it.

> In Rally's version of tag, everyone is it and nobody has to call IT.
