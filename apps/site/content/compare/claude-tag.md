**Short answer:** If your company already runs on Claude Team or Enterprise and lives in Slack, Claude Tag is a strong, natural add. Anyone in a channel can hand @Claude real work and watch it happen. Rally is the better pick when you want the work to have a home of its own. Spaces hold the files and decisions, Breakouts give you room to think in private, and the organization pays the same amount every month. You can still reach Rally from Slack when that's handier.

## Where Claude Tag shines

### Nothing new to open

If your whole day already happens in Slack, tagging @Claude asks nothing of anyone. Anyone in a paired workspace can use it in channels, with no per-user setup.

### It takes on real work

Claude Tag runs tasks in its own sandbox and can open a pull request, query a data warehouse, or turn a long thread into a decision doc. It posts a checklist as it goes, so the channel can follow along and steer.

### Admins get fine-grained control

Owners decide which tools each channel can reach, set spend limits per channel and for the whole organization, and get alerts before a limit is hit. For a big company, that kind of control matters.

## Where Rally wins

### A bill you can say out loud

Rally is $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with one shared quota and no seat limit. Your finance person can put it in the budget once and move on.

### The work has a home

A Space holds the files, decisions, and history for a project, and Channels keep each conversation in its place. Rally works from that history, and the drafts and dashboards it makes are saved right there in the Channel.

### No setup project

Nobody writes system prompts, skills files, or plugins. When a job needs a specialty, like your brand voice or a follow-up after every sales call, Rally creates a Sidekick for itself and keeps using it.

### It knows who can see what

Rally keeps track of who asked what and who is allowed to see what, across people and connectors. A shared AI stays shared without oversharing, and Breakouts let you rough something out before the whole team sees it.

> In Rally's version of tag, everyone is it and nobody has to call IT.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Claude Tag if

- Your company is already on Claude Team or Enterprise
- Your team does nearly everything inside Slack
- You have an admin who wants to set tools and spend limits channel by channel

### Pick Rally if

- You want a fixed monthly price for the whole team
- You want projects that keep their context in one place
- You want shared Channels and private Breakouts with the same AI
- You'd rather the AI set up its own Sidekicks than configure skills and plugins

## Questions

### How much does Claude Tag cost compared to Rally?

Claude Tag needs a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, and work in Slack channels is billed by usage from a balance your organization funds, under a spend limit an Owner sets. Rally charges the organization a flat monthly price. Start is $100 and Grow is $250, and the whole team shares one quota.

### Can I use Rally from Slack like Claude Tag?

Yes. Install the Rally app, then mention @Rally in a thread, DM it, or use /rally. Rally posts progress back to Slack, and the full history stays in Rally, inside the Space and Channel the work belongs to. So the team can keep chatting in Slack while the project keeps its memory.

### Does Rally only use one AI model?

No. Rally talks to you in one consistent voice, but it routes each job to the model family best suited to it. You are not locked to a single vendor, and nobody on your team has to choose a model before asking a question.

### How do I try Rally?

Download the macOS desktop beta directly, with no waitlist. You get a 14 day free trial with no credit card. Invite two coworkers during the trial and it stretches to 60 days, which is plenty of time to see how a team works with one shared AI.
