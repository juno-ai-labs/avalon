If you already have both Claude Team and Slack, Claude Tag seems a perfect fit. In fact, we believe it's a great fit for software development startups since Claude Tag was an internal project made public. Rally becomes the stronger product when real-time multi-human collaboration is how you want to work as a team.

## Side by side

| | Rally | Claude Tag |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Built for | Teams who work with one shared AI | Claude Team and Enterprise customers who work in Slack |
| People in one AI conversation | Yes. Everyone in a Canvas talks to the same Rally. | Yes. One Claude per Slack channel that anyone there can tag. |
| Shared context | Each Canvas keeps its conversation, files, and versions together for people and AI alike. | Slack threads, plus memory Claude keeps per channel and workspace. |
| Private side work | Every Canvas starts private to you. Share it with a teammate, a guest, or everyone. | Direct messages, which run on your own Claude seat. |
| Your tools | Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Granola, and more. Reads and writes back. | Connections, plugins, and skills an admin sets up per channel or workspace. |
| Runs on its own | Sidekicks run on a schedule or when something happens. | Yes. Scheduled jobs, channel watching, and triggers. |
| Models | One voice, with each job routed to the model family best at it. | Claude. |
| Pricing | $100 or $250 per organization a month. No seat limit. | Needs a Team or Enterprise plan. Channel work is billed by usage, under a spend limit. |

## Where Rally wins

### A bill you can say out loud

Rally is $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with one shared quota and no seat limit. Your finance person can put it in the budget once and move on.

### The work has a home

Each piece of work gets its own Canvas, with the conversation beside the thing Rally made from it. Rally works from that history, and every draft and dashboard it makes is saved there as a version.

### No setup project

Nobody writes system prompts, skills files, or plugins. When a job keeps coming back, like a follow-up after every sales call, you describe it in plain words and Rally builds a Sidekick for it that the whole team shares.

### It knows who can see what

Rally keeps track of who asked what and who is allowed to see what, across people and connectors. A shared AI stays shared without oversharing, and a new Canvas stays yours until you share it, so you can rough something out before the whole team sees it.

> In Rally's version of tag, everyone is it and nobody has to call IT.
