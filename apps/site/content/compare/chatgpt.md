ChatGPT when it's you and AI (and never anyone else). Rally when the work gets better with a team. In Rally, your team and AI talk in a shared Canvas, right beside the thing you are making together.

> Already started something in ChatGPT? Paste it into a new Canvas and carry on with your team from there.

## Side by side

|                               | Rally                                                                                   | ChatGPT                                                                 |

## Where Rally wins

### One conversation, the whole team in it

Everyone in a Canvas talks to the same Rally, together. Rally keeps track of who asked what, so a question from Maya and a follow-up from Sam land in the same place, with the same AI and the same history.

### Context that stays where the work is

A Canvas keeps the conversation, the files, and every version of the work in one place. When a teammate jumps in on Thursday, Rally already knows what happened on Monday, and nobody has to retell the story or paste it over.

### Private when you want it, shared when it's ready

Need to think something through alone or with one coworker? Every Canvas starts private to you, so do the messy part there and share it when it's ready. Rally knows who is allowed to see what, across people and connected tools alike.

### One price, however many people join

Rally is priced per organization, with one quota the team shares. Bring in the designer and the contractor without doing seat math first.
