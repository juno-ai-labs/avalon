**Short answer:** ChatGPT when it's you and the AI. Rally when the work belongs to a team. ChatGPT is the best known assistant around, and it keeps adding team features like shared projects and the new Space. Most of the work still happens one person to one chat, though. In Rally, your team and one AI talk in the same Channel, and the files and past decisions sit right beside the conversation. Whoever picks the work up next finds it where it was left. The price covers the whole organization.

## Where ChatGPT shines

### Everyone already knows it

Your coworkers have probably used ChatGPT today. Nobody needs a tour, and the free plan means anyone can start in seconds.

### A huge amount in one app

Voice, images, a desktop app with Codex built in, and the ChatGPT Work agent, which can work for hours on long tasks across your files and apps.

### A great thinking partner

For brainstorms, drafts, quick research, or a second look at an email, ChatGPT is fast and very good at it.

## Where Rally wins

### One conversation, the whole team in it

Everyone in a Channel talks to the same Rally, together. Rally keeps track of who asked what, so a question from Maya and a follow-up from Sam land in the same place, with the same AI and the same history.

### Context that stays where the work is

A Space keeps the files, decisions, and past conversations for a project. When a teammate jumps in on Thursday, Rally already knows what happened on Monday, and nobody has to retell the story or paste it over.

### Private when you want it, shared when it's ready

Need to think something through alone or with one coworker? Open a Breakout, do the messy part there, and merge it back when it's ready. Rally knows who is allowed to see what, across people and connected tools alike.

### One price, however many people join

Rally is priced per organization, with one quota the team shares. Bring in the designer and the contractor without doing seat math first.

> Before Rally, our team's AI process was pure GPT. Grab it, Paste it, Tell everyone.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick ChatGPT if

- It's mostly you, working on your own
- You want voice, images, and a personal agent in one familiar app
- You need Windows or Microsoft 365 support today

### Pick Rally if

- Your work needs more than one person and one AI in the same conversation
- You want context that carries over when work changes hands
- You want private side work that folds back into the shared thread
- You'd rather pay one price for the team than count seats

## Questions

### Does ChatGPT still have group chats?

Not for new work. OpenAI began winding group chats down on July 9, 2026. You can no longer start one or join one, and existing ones are becoming read-only. OpenAI points teams to shared projects instead, where each person's chats sit side by side. In Rally, the shared conversation is the core of the product: everyone in a Channel talks to the same Rally, together.

### Is ChatGPT Business cheaper than Rally for a small team?

At four people they cost about the same. ChatGPT Business is $25 a seat a month, or $20 billed yearly, with a two-seat minimum, so four seats is $80 to $100 a month. Rally Start is $100 a month for the whole organization. Each new ChatGPT seat adds to the bill, while Rally's price stays flat until you move to Grow at $250 a month.

### What is ChatGPT Space, and is it like Rally?

Space launched on September 29, 2026 for Pro, Business, and Enterprise. It's a shared home for pages and files that teammates can edit together, and each person works with their own ChatGPT. Rally starts from the conversation instead: your team and one AI talk in the same Channel, and the documents it makes are saved right there.

### Which AI models does Rally use?

Rally keeps one consistent voice and sends each job to the model family best suited to it. It isn't locked to one vendor, and nobody on the team has to pick a model from a menu.
