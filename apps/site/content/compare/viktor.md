**Short answer:** Viktor is a good call if you want to hand errands to an AI hire inside Slack or Teams and you're comfortable paying per task in credits. When the work needs more than one person, Rally is the better fit. In Rally your team and the AI sit in the same Channel, with the project's files and decisions right there, so a teammate can step in without a briefing. It still answers you in Slack when you mention it. And the price is flat per organization, so nobody thinks twice before asking.

## Where Viktor shines

### A huge tool catalog

Viktor lists more than 3,200 integrations, from Stripe and HubSpot to Google Ads and Meta Ads. If your team runs on something niche, there's a good chance it's covered.

### Slack or Teams, your pick

It lives where your team already chats, and it works in Microsoft Teams as well as Slack. There's a browser app too, for longer chats.

### Big errands from one message

Ask once and it can come back with a PDF report, a spreadsheet, or a small web app. Plenty of early teams have taken to it fast: Viktor says more than 12,000 teams installed it within months of launch.

## Where Rally wins

### Flat, so people just ask

Rally costs one flat amount per organization, with one shared quota. Start is $100 a month and Grow is $250. The tenth question of the day costs the same as the first.

### Next week starts where this one ended

A Space keeps its Channels, files, and decisions together, and Rally reads all of it. Next week, a new teammate or the AI starts from where the team left off, without anyone re-explaining.

### Working it out together

Handing off a task is useful. Shaping it together is better. Three people and Rally can work one draft in the same Channel, see how it got there, and step into a Breakout when someone needs to think alone before merging back.

### Roles that set themselves up

When a job keeps coming back, like a weekly digest or a follow-up after every sales call, Rally creates a Sidekick for it. Nobody writes prompts or config. It runs in the cloud on a schedule or when something happens, and it knows who asked and who is allowed to see what.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Viktor if

- Your team lives in Microsoft Teams
- You need a niche integration connected today
- You mostly want to send a task and get a finished file back

### Pick Rally if

- Your team and the AI should work from the same project context
- You want a flat monthly price, with no meter on each request
- You want shared Channels plus private Breakouts that merge back
- You want recurring roles that set themselves up and run with laptops closed

## Questions

### Is Rally an AI employee like Viktor?

Not quite. Rally is a shared place to work where one AI is part of the team. You can hand it a task and it will do it, reading and writing in tools like Gmail, Notion, GitHub, and Linear. The bigger difference is that the team works alongside it in Channels, with the same files and history, so the work doesn't live only in a chat thread.

### How does Rally pricing compare to Viktor credits?

Rally is a flat price per organization. Start is $100 a month and suits teams under five, Grow is $250 a month and suits teams under fifteen, and there's no seat limit. Viktor sells shared workspace credits, from $50 for 20,000 a month, and its own guide puts one quick task at 100 to 300 credits and a full project at 2,000 to 5,000.

### Can I use Rally from Slack like Viktor?

Yes. With the Rally app in Slack, you can mention @Rally in a channel, send it a DM, or type /rally. Progress posts back to Slack while the full history stays in Rally, next to the project's files and decisions. Rally doesn't support Microsoft Teams today, so Teams-first companies will find Viktor a closer fit for now.

### Does Rally have a free trial?

Yes. Rally comes with a 14 day trial and no credit card. Invite two coworkers and the trial grows to 60 days. Rally is a macOS desktop beta you can download directly, with no waitlist.
