**Short answer:** Viktor is a good call if you want to hand errands to an AI hire inside Slack or Teams and you're comfortable paying per task in credits. When the work needs more than one person, Rally is the better fit. In Rally your team and the AI sit in the same Canvas, with the work's files and versions right there, so a teammate can step in without a briefing. A Sidekick can still watch Slack and post its results back there. And the price is flat per organization, so nobody thinks twice before asking.

## Where Rally wins

### Flat, so people just ask

Rally costs one flat amount per organization, with one shared quota. Start is $100 a month and Grow is $250. The tenth question of the day costs the same as the first.

### Next week starts where this one ended

A Canvas keeps its conversation, files, and versions together, and Rally reads all of it. Next week, a new teammate or the AI starts from where the team left off, without anyone re-explaining.

### Working it out together

Handing off a task is useful. Shaping it together is better. Three people and Rally can work one draft in the same Canvas, see how it got there, and leave comments right on the page. Anyone who needs to think alone first can start a private Canvas of their own.

### Roles that set themselves up

When a job keeps coming back, like a weekly summary or a follow-up after every sales call, you describe it in plain words and Rally builds a Sidekick for it. Nobody writes prompts or config. It runs in the cloud on a schedule or when something happens, and it knows who asked and who is allowed to see what.
