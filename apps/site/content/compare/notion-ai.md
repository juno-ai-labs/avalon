**Short answer:** If your team's work already lives in Notion, Notion AI is a strong helper for writing, searching, and automating inside it. Rally is for the work that happens before anything gets written down. Your team and one AI share the same conversation in a Channel, with context pulled from Gmail, Drive, Slack, GitHub, Linear, and Notion too, and Rally writes back to those tools. Keep Notion as the wiki and use Rally to do the work. The price is set per organization, not per member.

## Where Notion AI shines

### Right inside your pages

Drafting, rewriting, translating, and filling in database properties all happen on the page you're already looking at. Nothing to copy and paste.

### Agents that know your workspace

Notion Agent can work through many steps at once across pages and databases, creating or updating hundreds of pages in one go. If your projects live in Notion databases, it knows its way around them.

### A choice of top models

You can pick GPT, Claude, or Gemini for a task, or let Notion choose. Admins can decide which models the workspace can use.

## Where Rally wins

### Room for the contractor

Rally costs $100 or $250 a month for the organization, and everyone draws on one shared quota. Bring in a contractor or a new hire without checking a credit balance first.

### Answers from all your tools

Rally reads Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Slack, GitHub, Linear, Granola, and Notion, and writes back to them. It's permission-aware across people and connectors, so each person only gets what they're allowed to see.

### Help that sets itself up

Ask for a weekly digest or a follow-up after every sales call, and Rally creates a Sidekick for the job. Nobody writes instructions or prompts, and it runs in the cloud with your laptop closed.

### Several people, one AI, same room

Work usually starts as a discussion. In a Rally Channel, three teammates and the AI talk it through together, Rally keeps track of who asked what, and the finished doc or dashboard is saved right there in the Channel.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Notion AI if

- Your team already lives in Notion and wants AI right on the page
- Most of your automation is about Notion databases
- You're happy paying per member and adding credits for agents

### Pick Rally if

- Your work is spread across Gmail, Drive, Slack, GitHub, and more
- You want several people and one AI working in the same conversation
- You want recurring jobs handled without anyone writing agent instructions
- You want a price set for the organization instead of each member

## Questions

### Does Rally replace Notion?

No, and it doesn't need to. Rally connects to Notion, reads your pages, and writes back to them. Keep Notion as the wiki and the database of record, and use Rally for the conversations and tasks that produce what ends up there. You get one AI working across Notion and the rest of your tools.

### Can Rally work with my Notion pages?

Yes. Connect Notion and Rally can read and update your pages alongside Gmail, Drive, Slack, GitHub, and the rest. Rally is permission-aware across people and connectors, so it tracks who is allowed to see what and keeps private pages private.

### How does Rally pricing compare to Notion AI?

Notion's full AI comes with the Business plan at $20 per member a month billed yearly, within a usage allowance, and Custom Agents use Notion credits at $10 per 1,000. Rally charges per organization: $100 a month on Start and $250 on Grow, with no seat limit and one shared quota. The trial is 14 days with no credit card.

### Does Rally run on Windows?

Not today. Rally is a macOS desktop app in beta, with a direct download and no waitlist. It connects to Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Granola, and more. If you need something else, tell us.
