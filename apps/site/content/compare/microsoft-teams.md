**Short answer:** Teams is the natural home for a company on Microsoft 365, and its meetings are excellent. For a small team that wants to do its work together with AI, Rally is the easier fit. In Rally, everyone in a Channel talks to the same AI, and the project's documents and past decisions live right next to the chat. It connects to Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, GitHub, and more. And it costs one price for the organization, so nobody has to decide which seats get the AI.

## Where Microsoft Teams shines

### Big, long meetings

Even the $4 Teams Essentials plan runs meetings of up to 30 hours with up to 300 people. With Copilot, meeting recaps can be translated into the language you pick.

### It comes with Microsoft 365

If your company already pays for Microsoft 365, Teams is on every laptop, and Copilot Chat comes with it for every user at no extra cost.

### AI inside the Office apps

With a Copilot license, the same assistant works across Word, Excel, Outlook, and Teams, and Copilot Chat lets you pick Anthropic's Claude from its model selector.

## Where Rally wins

### One price, everybody in

Rally costs $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with no seat limit and one shared quota. The AI comes with it, so there's no second license to buy and no list of who gets it.

### Everyone talks to the same AI

In a Rally Channel, the whole team works with one AI, and it keeps track of who asked what and who may see what, across people and connectors. Someone who joins halfway through can scroll up and carry on.

### A home for each project

A Space holds a project's files, decisions, and artifacts. Need to sort something out with one coworker first? Open a Breakout, then merge it back into the Channel when it's ready.

### Download it and start

Grab the macOS beta, invite two coworkers, and connect Gmail, Google Drive, Slack, Notion, or GitHub. Ask for a weekly digest and Rally makes a Sidekick for it, which keeps running in the cloud after you close your laptop.

> Why did the Sidekick skip the meeting? It already had the minutes.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Microsoft Teams if

- Your company already runs on Microsoft 365 and Windows
- Video meetings are the center of your day
- Your IT team needs everything inside one Microsoft admin center

### Pick Rally if

- You want your whole team working with one AI on the same task
- Your team lives in Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, or GitHub
- You want the AI included for everyone at one price
- You want to download it and get going, with no waitlist

## Questions

### Is Copilot free in Microsoft Teams?

Partly. Copilot Chat is included for Microsoft 365 users at no extra cost. The deeper features, like the Teams channel agent and Facilitator, need a Microsoft 365 Copilot license on top of a Microsoft 365 plan. For organizations of up to 300 people, Copilot Business lists at $21 per user a month. Rally includes its AI in one organization price.

### How much does Rally cost compared to Teams with Copilot?

Rally is $100 a month on Start and $250 on Grow, for the whole organization, with the AI included and no seat limit. On the Microsoft side, a 10 person team on Business Basic at $7 plus Copilot Business at $21 pays about $280 a month at list price. A 14 day Rally trial needs no credit card.

### Does Rally work with Microsoft Teams or Microsoft 365?

Not today. Rally is a macOS desktop app in beta. It connects to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Granola, Fireflies, and more, and it can reach other tools through custom MCP servers and inbound webhooks.

### Can Rally replace Teams meetings?

No. Rally doesn't host video calls. It's where the work around the meeting happens: it reads notes from Granola or Fireflies, drafts the follow-ups, and keeps the decisions with the project, where the whole team and the AI can find them.
