**Short answer:** Keep Slack, and add Rally for the work itself. Slack is the best place for quick team chat, and Slackbot is now a capable personal AI agent. But Slackbot works with one person at a time. In Rally, your whole team and one AI share the same conversation and files inside a Canvas, so the next person can carry on without a recap. Rally works from Slack too: a Sidekick can watch a channel, start a run when something is said there, and post its results back to the thread. The price is per organization, not per person.

## Where Rally wins

### One AI the whole team shares

In a Rally Canvas, everyone talks to the same AI. It tracks who asked what and who is allowed to see what, across people and connectors, so a teammate can jump in mid-task and find the whole story there.

### Work that stays put

A Canvas keeps a piece of work's conversation and files together, along with every version of the drafts and dashboards Rally makes. Chat can scroll on by. The work stays where you left it.

### Jobs that run themselves

Describe a weekly summary in plain words and Rally builds a Sidekick for it. There's no workflow to build or prompt to write, and it keeps going in the cloud when laptops close.

### A bill that ignores headcount

Rally costs $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with no seat limit and one shared quota. Add the intern to the Canvas and the invoice doesn't notice.
