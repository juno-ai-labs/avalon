**Short answer:** Keep Slack, and add Rally for the work itself. Slack is the best place for quick team chat, and Slackbot is now a capable personal AI agent. But Slackbot works with one person at a time. In Rally, your whole team and one AI share the same conversation and files inside a Space, so the next person can carry on without a recap. You can reach Rally from Slack too. Mention @Rally, DM it, or type /rally, and progress posts back to the thread. The price is per organization, not per person.

## Where Slack shines

### Everyone is already there

Your team, your contractors, and often your customers. More than 350,000 organizations work together in shared Slack Connect channels.

### An app for everything

With over 2,600 apps in the Marketplace, whatever tool you use probably posts into Slack already.

### AI on every plan

Summaries, recaps, AI search, and Slackbot now come with Slack itself, and Business+ gets the full Slackbot with no add-on to buy.

## Where Rally wins

### One AI the whole team shares

In a Rally Channel, everyone talks to the same AI. It tracks who asked what and who is allowed to see what, across people and connectors, so a teammate can jump in mid-task and find the whole story there.

### Work that stays put

A Space keeps a project's files and decisions together, along with the drafts and dashboards Rally makes. Chat can scroll on by. The work stays where you left it.

### Jobs that run themselves

Ask for a weekly digest and Rally creates a Sidekick for it. There's no workflow to build or prompt to write, and it keeps going in the cloud when laptops close.

### A bill that ignores headcount

Rally costs $100 or $250 a month for the whole organization, with no seat limit and one shared quota. Add the intern to the Channel and the invoice doesn't notice.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Slack if

- You need fast, casual chat across your company and with outside partners
- A personal AI that catches each person up is all the AI you need
- You rely on a long tail of apps that post into chat

### Pick Rally if

- You want your team and one AI working on the same task together
- You want each project's files and decisions kept together
- You want recurring jobs handled without anyone building a workflow
- You'd like the bill to stay the same when someone joins

## Questions

### Do I have to leave Slack to use Rally?

No. Add the Rally app to your Slack workspace and talk to it there by mentioning @Rally in a thread, sending a DM, or typing /rally. Progress posts back to Slack and the full history stays in Rally, so anyone on the team can open it later with all the context. Keep both. Slack for chat, Rally for the work.

### Isn't Slackbot enough?

For one person, often yes. Slackbot can summarize, search, draft, and run tasks for you. Rally is built for the moment a second person joins: the whole team shares one AI in a Channel, with shared files and history, Breakouts for side work, and Sidekicks that run jobs on a schedule or when something happens.

### How does Rally pricing compare to Slack?

Slack charges per user: $7.25 a month on Pro and $15 on Business+ when billed yearly. Rally charges per organization: $100 a month on Start and $250 on Grow, with no seat limit. A 14 day trial needs no credit card, and inviting two coworkers stretches it to 60 days.

### Does Rally work on Windows or with Microsoft Teams?

Not yet on either. Rally is a macOS desktop app in beta, and it connects to Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, GitHub, Linear, Granola, and more.
