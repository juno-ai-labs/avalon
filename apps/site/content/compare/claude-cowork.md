**Short answer:** Claude Cowork is great when the work is yours alone. Once it belongs to a team, Rally is the better fit. Cowork is a capable agent. Give it a goal and it works through your folders, apps, and connectors, and scheduled tasks can now run in Anthropic's cloud. Each session belongs to one person's account, though. Rally starts from the team instead. Coworkers and one AI share Channels, the Space remembers the project for everyone, and the organization pays once for all of it.

## Where Claude Cowork shines

### It does the busywork for you

Give Cowork a goal and it plans the steps, then works across your files and tools. Reconciling a spreadsheet or drafting a weekly report from a folder of notes is exactly its kind of job.

### It reaches your own computer

Cowork can work in local folders and apps, and it has its own browser panel for forms and research. If the files live on your laptop, it can get to them.

### It goes where you go

Cowork now runs on the web, on desktop, and on mobile in beta. Scheduled tasks keep running in the cloud with your laptop closed, and it comes with any paid Claude plan.

## Where Rally wins

### Teammates can jump in

Everyone in a Channel talks to the same Rally. Someone can add context, push back, or pick up where you left off, and Rally keeps track of who asked what.

### A quota the team shares

Rally is priced per organization, and everyone draws on the same quota. Adding the person who needs to see the plan costs nothing extra.

### Results land where people look

Artifacts are saved in the Channel, whether it's a short doc or a full public dashboard. The latest version is where everyone already works, and Breakouts merge private drafts back when they're ready.

### Sidekicks that react

Sidekicks run on a schedule or when something happens, like a new sales call or an inbound webhook. Rally creates them as the work calls for them. Nobody has to write a system prompt, and they keep running in the cloud.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Claude Cowork if

- The work is yours alone and nobody else needs the context
- The files you need live in folders on your own computer
- You already pay for Claude and want an agent inside it

### Pick Rally if

- The work involves two or more people
- You want one AI the whole team talks to, with shared history
- You want one price for the organization instead of a seat for each person
- You want jobs that start when something happens, not only on a timer

## Questions

### Can I use Claude Cowork and Rally together?

Yes, and it's a sensible split. Keep a personal agent for solo chores and use Rally for team work. If the result matters to more than one person, do it in a Rally Channel, so the context, the decisions, and the final artifact stay with the team instead of in one person's account.

### Is Claude Cowork still a separate product?

Not for long. In September 2026 Anthropic announced it is folding Cowork into the main Claude app, so one conversation can handle quick questions and longer tasks. It is rolling out to Pro and Max first, with Team and Free plans following. The agent abilities stay; the separate mode goes away.

### Does Rally work on my local files?

Rally works with the tools your team already shares: Google Drive, Gmail, Notion, GitHub, Slack, Linear, and more, plus custom MCP servers. That's on purpose. Shared work should live where the whole team can reach it, and Rally respects each person's access to those files and threads.

### How does pricing compare for a team of ten?

Rally's Grow plan is $250 a month for the organization, with no seat limit and one shared quota. Claude bills Cowork through per-seat plans, so ten people on Team Standard is $250 a month at the monthly rate, and Team Premium costs more. With Rally, the eleventh person costs nothing extra.
