**Short answer:** Claude Cowork is great when the work is yours alone. Once it belongs to a team, Rally is the better fit. Cowork is a capable agent. Give it a goal and it works through your folders, apps, and connectors, and scheduled tasks can now run in Anthropic's cloud. Each session belongs to one person's account, though. Rally starts from the team instead. Coworkers and one AI share Channels, the Space remembers the project for everyone, and the organization pays once for all of it.

## Where Rally wins

### Teammates can jump in

Everyone in a Channel talks to the same Rally. Someone can add context, push back, or pick up where you left off, and Rally keeps track of who asked what.

### A quota the team shares

Rally is priced per organization, and everyone draws on the same quota. Adding the person who needs to see the plan costs nothing extra.

### Results land where people look

Artifacts are saved in the Channel, whether it's a short doc or a full public dashboard. The latest version is where everyone already works, and Breakouts merge private drafts back when they're ready.

### Sidekicks that react

Sidekicks run on a schedule or when something happens, like a new sales call or an inbound webhook. Rally creates them as the work calls for them. Nobody has to write a system prompt, and they keep running in the cloud.
