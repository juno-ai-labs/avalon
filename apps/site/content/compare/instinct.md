**Short answer:** Pick Instinct for your own life admin, and Rally for your team's work. Instinct is a personal agent you text or call. It books travel and restaurants, orders groceries, pays bills, and follows up on threads you dropped. It is built around one person. Rally is built around a team. Your coworkers and one AI share Spaces and Channels, the AI knows who is allowed to see what, and Sidekicks keep running in the cloud after everyone's laptop is shut. Different jobs. Instinct for your weekend, Rally for your week.

## Where Instinct shines

### It does the errand

Instinct doesn't stop at a suggestion. It books the table, checks you in, orders the groceries, and cancels the subscription you forgot about.

### Text it like a friend

There's nothing to learn. You text it or call it, and it can reach out to you first when something needs your attention.

### It picks up the phone

With its own phone number and a new Concierge service, it can call places that have no online booking at all.

## Where Rally wins

### Work your team can see

In Rally, the plan, the draft, and the back-and-forth with the AI live in a shared Channel. Coworkers can read what was asked and by whom, so nobody has to take it on faith.

### Built for many people's permissions

Rally tracks who is allowed to see which files and threads, across people and connectors. A shared AI that respects each person's access is a different thing from one person's keys.

### One project, one place

Each Space is one project. Its files and past decisions live there, with Channels for each conversation and Breakouts for side work. Want the status of the launch? Open the launch Channel.

### Start today, at a known price

No waitlist and no invite code. Download the Mac beta, start a 14 day trial with no credit card, and know your price up front: $100 or $250 per organization a month.

> Instinct will run your errands. Rally runs the errand-tire team.

## Which one should you pick?

### Pick Instinct if

- You want help with personal errands, bookings, and bills
- You like handing off life admin over text or a phone call
- You already have an invite

### Pick Rally if

- The work belongs to a team, not one person
- You want coworkers and the AI in the same conversation
- You need an AI that respects each person's access
- You want to start today at a published price

## Questions

### Is Instinct good for teams at work?

Instinct is built as a personal agent for one person's life, and its own pages describe errands, bookings, and inbox follow-ups. Its newer agent-to-agent feature helps friends coordinate plans. For a team sharing projects, files, and decisions with one AI, Rally is built for that job, with Spaces, Channels, and permissions for every person in the room.

### How do I get access to Instinct?

Instinct is invite-only for now. You join the waitlist at app.instinct.com or get an invite link from an existing user. Rally has no waitlist: any team on a Mac can download the beta and start a 14 day trial without a credit card, and inviting two coworkers stretches the trial to 60 days.

### Can Rally book travel or order groceries?

That's not what we built it for. Rally does team work: drafts, research, plans, follow-ups, and dashboards, with your coworkers in the loop and your work tools connected. For groceries, a personal agent or a list on the fridge will serve you better.

### How much does Instinct cost compared to Rally?

Instinct has no published price and is free during its invite-only beta, with no word yet on what it will cost later. Rally costs $100 a month per organization on Start or $250 on Grow, with no seat limit and one shared quota, so adding a teammate never changes the bill.
