# Company philosophy: labor abundance

This is Juno Labs' statement of why Rally exists. It is the thing every product
decision is checked against, and the source the launch statement is written
from. It is not an engineering document; it changes only when the company's
belief changes.

## The belief

**Work is done by teams. Teams are not going anywhere. You are the future of
your work, not an agent.**

Not all work, but most work that matters depends on more than one person. The
best work is done collaboratively. Teams beat individuals. None of that changed
when AI arrived, and Rally is built on the bet that it will not.

## What is broken today

Most of us are not AI optimizers. We do not want a better prompt. We want to
get our work done. Here is how that actually goes right now:

1. Someone gets the ball rolling in their own Claude or ChatGPT.
2. They hand a distillation to the next person, who reviews it or builds on it.
3. That rally between people happens in isolated rooms. Your context and your
   tools do not match mine, so we each get different answers from our own AI,
   and someone has to reconcile them by hand.

That is a waste of time. The team's work is collaborative, but the tools are
single-player. Chat apps are built for people talking to people, and their
structure does not hold up once agents are doing real work inside the
conversation. Copying AI output into Slack is the symptom.

## What we reject

**The future of work is not AI personal assistants.** An assistant per person
mostly makes more work for that person: more drafts to read, more options to
weigh, more decisions to make. Call it decision multiplication. Multiplying
every teammate's output by an agent and then asking humans to merge the
results is not abundance. It is more of the same waste, faster.

## What we build instead

**Labor is driven by humans. Agents are the fuel.** Rally puts the team and the
agent in one shared, durable place, so the ball gets rolled once, in the open,
with the same context and the same tools for everyone. The conversation, the
assignment, and the result stay together. People still set the direction,
make the calls, and own the outcome. Agents make that team faster.

Abundance means a team can take on more than its headcount used to allow. It
does not mean fewer people.

## How this shows up in the product

- **Work lives in Channels, not in someone's private chat.** A Channel is the
  durable, multiplayer home for a task, and Rally is present in every one of
  them. There is no personal AI to copy out of.
- **Shared context and shared tools.** Everyone in a Channel gets answers from
  the same context, skills, and connectors, so there is nothing to reconcile.
- **Sidekicks are delegation, not replacement.** A Sidekick is work a person
  handed off. It runs in a Channel the team can see, and its results land where
  the team already is.
- **Rally never asks a person to confirm each tool call.** Fuel does not ask
  for permission to burn. Authentication, capability checks, and receipts keep
  it safe; humans stay in charge of the work, not the busywork.
- **Nothing is designed for the AI striver.** The product must earn its place
  with someone who just wants the work done and would rather not think about
  the model at all.

## The test for a decision

When a feature, a design, or a piece of copy is on the table, ask:

1. Does this make the team faster, or only one person's assistant busier?
2. Does the work stay in the shared room, or does it leak back into private
   chats?
3. Does a person still own the outcome, or did we quietly hand it to an agent?

If the answer to any of these is the wrong one, it is not Rally.
