The minimum version, on the 5c Thread-first, no bubble Channel feed. One representation everywhere: X merged into Y, with a short piece of markdown that can embed artifacts. In a Thread it is an ordinary message; in a Channel it is a Channel message, arriving at the bottom when it was merged, so both stay in time order. A person merges with the composer, or asks Rally to. Reasoning is in the PRD.
