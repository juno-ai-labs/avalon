Explorations for merging a Breakout's result into its Thread and a Thread's result into its Channel. The one new object: an Outcome

A Breakout opens with ↳ and is shown in its pane with a sunken origin card. The Outcome mirrors both: it comes back with ↰ and is drawn as the same sunken card, so it reads as the other end of something the app already shows.
