Merge-back on the new Channel feed

A second set, seeded from the 5c Thread-first, no bubble Channel feed. In 5c a Channel reads like a conversation: a thread goes out on the right like a sent message, and its result comes back on the left like a reply, under a "↰" line quoting what was asked. Everything here extends that one pattern to statuses, decisions, privacy, revisions, the Thread level, and the composer that makes a merge. The first set and the design document still stand.
