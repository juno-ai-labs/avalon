Merge and Breakout suggestions

After Rally's turn settles, one quiet chip can appear under that turn: merge this up, or start a Breakout on the side question. It is drawn only while the turn is one of the newest three messages, then it hides for good. Accepting a merge opens a response card in the feed, and every merge is made in that card now: the composer is never involved. Drawn in the same 5c Thread-first, no bubble language as the merge-back design, with the app's own hover row, breakout chips, merge mode and launch pane, so the line is judged beside what it joins. Reasoning is in the PRD and the mechanics in the design.

Where it sits

Under the turn it is about, after the turn's attachments and its hover row, in the row where "Breakouts from this message" chips are drawn: a dashed chip with Rally's mark, a title, one sentence of context, one cut line of the words it would post or start with, one action and a dismiss.

Quiet

What a suggestion does not do is most of the design.

Open

One chip under the turn, for the people who can act, while the turn is among the newest three. Nothing in the composer, no unread, no Activity row, no push.

Accepting

The response card is open in the feed where the chip was, or the launch pane is open. Nothing is posted yet.

Accepted

The merge lands or the Breakout is created, through the same admission as today. The chip resolves on its own.

Dismissed

Gone for everyone in the conversation, at once. Recorded with who, so the same kind rests here for a while.

Aged out

Three newer messages arrived. Hidden for good, whatever the classifier still thinks. Nobody acted: recorded as ignored.

Cannot act

A viewer who cannot merge or cannot post sees no chip. A private thread never gets the Channel one.

Shadow

Phase 1. Every decision is recorded; no chip is drawn anywhere until the threshold is measured.
