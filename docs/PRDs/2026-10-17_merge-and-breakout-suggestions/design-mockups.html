Quiet

What a suggestion does not do is most of the design. These are the states, and one board for the common one.

Open

One line in the composer, for the people who can act. Nothing in the feed, no unread, no Activity row, no push.

Accepted

The merge lands or the Breakout is created, through the same admission as today. The line resolves on its own.

Dismissed

Gone for everyone in the conversation, at once. Recorded with who, so the same kind rests here for a while.

Superseded

A new message or a new run replaces it with whatever the next judgement says, which is usually nothing. Nobody acted: recorded as ignored.

Cannot act

A viewer who cannot merge or cannot post sees no line. A private thread never gets the Channel one.

Shadow

Phase 1. Every decision is recorded; no line is drawn anywhere until the threshold is measured.
