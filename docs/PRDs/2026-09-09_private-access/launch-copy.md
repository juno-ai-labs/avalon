## Personal posts

Look, I’ll be honest.

I’ve been working in AI for years, and still, when someone sends me a GitHub repo
or folder with a great skill they built, it sits in an open tab for weeks.

I WANT to use it.

It’s just weirdly overwhelming to get someone else’s AI setup working on my own.

That’s the reason I built Rally — for both sides of that exchange.

They can share it once. I can use it immediately. We can even use it together.

No install instructions. No “I’ll get to this later.” No rebuilding it myself.

---

One thing that kept happening on our team:

“Can you send me the prompt you used for that?”

Sure.

Then I’d send the prompt, oh wait and the attached doc, oh wait and the
conversation behind it.

At that point, I’d basically reconstructed my private AI session over Slack.

It made me realize how much context gets trapped inside individual AI accounts.

We’re building Rally so the conversation, context, and people can live together
from the beginning.
