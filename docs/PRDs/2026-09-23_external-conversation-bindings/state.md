Partly superseded by [`2026-11-06_canvases-and-sidekicks`](../2026-11-06_canvases-and-sidekicks/prd.md)
on 2026-11-08: Slack no longer starts work, so the room binding that linked a
Slack conversation to a Space and opened a Channel per addressed thread (goals
1 to 4) was deleted, and a Sidekick's home conversation is now its own shared
conversation rather than a Channel or a Breakout (goal 7). What still holds:
a Sidekick run's connector send binds its reply thread to the run, and a reply
continues that run (goals 5 and 6), and the conversation tools, scoped to a
Sidekick's own conversation and its Canvases (goal 8, narrowed). See
[`external-conversation-bindings.md`](../../external-conversation-bindings.md)
and [`conversation-tools.md`](../../conversation-tools.md). Do not build room
bindings against this PRD.
