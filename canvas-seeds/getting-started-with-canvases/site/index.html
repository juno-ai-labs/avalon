Getting started

Welcome. This is a Canvas.

A Canvas is a conversation with Rally and the things it makes. This page is one of those things. It is live, your whole team shares it, and you change it by asking.

Six things to try

You have tried all six. The wall is your team's to keep.

  1. This page is a small app. It stores what people add, and it knows who you are because you opened it from Rally.

    • Add an idea to the wall
    • Vote for an idea
    Go to the wall

  2. Rally can read and write what this page stores. Ask in the Chat and the new cards show up on the wall a few seconds later. The page itself does not change.

    Paste it into the Chat beside this page and send it.

  3. You can show Rally the exact thing you mean.

    1. Turn on Comment mode. It is the pointer button above this page.
    2. Click the wall's title, Team wall.
    3. Type what you would rather call it and press Enter.
    4. Send the message in the Chat.

    Rally changes the title and publishes the page again.

  4. Rally changes a Canvas by publishing a new version. The version number above this page goes up, and the older versions stay there to look back at.

    One person does this for the whole team.

  5. Everyone in your Organization can open this Canvas. Ask a teammate to open it and add an idea to the wall. That ticks this step for the whole team.

    Then try this together in the Chat:

    1. Say hello to each other. Rally stays quiet.
    2. Ask for a change to the wall. Rally answers.

    Rally can tell which messages are meant for it, so nobody has to tag it. Talk to each other here as you would anywhere.

  6. Open Canvases, choose New Canvas, and say what you want to make. Or start from one of these:

    • A tracker for our hiring plan, with a row for each open role.
    • A launch checklist with an owner and a date for each task.
    • A weekly status page where each person posts what they shipped.
